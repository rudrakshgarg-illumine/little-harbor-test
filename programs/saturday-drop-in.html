Saturday Drop-In Program

Sometimes life needs a few extra hours. Maybe you have errands to run, a work commitment, a doctor's appointment, or you simply need a little time to recharge. Our Saturday Drop-In Program offers families flexible weekend care in a setting children will genuinely enjoy.

Where You'll Find It

The Saturday Drop-In Program is offered at one Little Harbor center:

  • Logan Square

Flexible Care When You Need It

Unlike our weekday programs, Saturday Drop-In is designed for families who need care on an occasional basis. It's a helpful option for parents with weekend work schedules, for families juggling a busy season, or for anyone who could use a breather. Contact our Logan Square center for details on how drop-in care works and how to reserve a spot.

A Saturday Kids Look Forward To

We want Saturday Drop-In to feel like a treat, not just a place to wait. Children spend their time in engaging, hands-on activities led by caring staff:

  • Art and making: painting, crafts, and creative building.
  • Stories and songs: read-alouds, music, and movement.
  • Active play: games and time to run off some energy.
  • Open play: blocks, pretend play, puzzles, and more.

Making Newcomers Feel at Home

Because drop-in care may bring together children who don't see each other every day, our staff pay special attention to helping everyone feel comfortable. We greet each child warmly, take time to learn their interests, and help them connect with others. Shy children get gentle support, and outgoing ones find plenty of friends to play with.

Same Little Harbor Care

Saturday Drop-In reflects the same values families trust during the week: safe spaces, thoughtful activities, and staff who treat children with kindness and respect. We'll share how your child's time went when you return.

Tell Us About Your Child

Before your child's first visit, we'll want to hear about their routines, allergies, comfort items, and anything else that helps us care for them well. The more we know, the smoother the day.
