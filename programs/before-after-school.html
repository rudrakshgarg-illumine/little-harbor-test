Before and After School Program

School days don't always line up neatly with work days. Our Before and After School Program gives school-age children a friendly, welcoming place to start and end their day, and gives parents the peace of mind of knowing their kids are with people who care.

Where You'll Find It

The Before and After School Program is offered at one Little Harbor center:

  • Naperville

A Calm Start to the Morning

Mornings can be hectic. Before school, children can ease into the day with quiet activities like reading, drawing, puzzles, and board games, or chat with friends before heading off to class. It's a relaxed transition that helps kids arrive at school ready to learn.

A Fun Finish to the Day

After a full day in the classroom, kids need a chance to move, unwind, and be themselves. Our after-school time balances energy and rest:

  • Active play: outdoor games, sports, and movement.
  • Creative time: art projects, building challenges, and music.
  • Homework support: a quiet space and a helping hand for assignments.
  • Downtime: books, games, and time to just hang out with friends.

Staff Who Get School-Age Kids

Older children need something different from preschoolers. Our staff give school-age kids more independence and more say in how they spend their time, while still providing guidance and supervision. Children help plan activities, take on leadership roles, and are trusted with real responsibility. That respect goes a long way.

A Familiar Community

For families who started with us in our early learning programs, before and after school care means children can stay connected to a place and people they already know. New families are warmly welcomed too, and kids quickly find their place.

Keeping Families in the Loop

Staff check in with families at pickup and are happy to share how the afternoon went, including how homework time went and anything worth celebrating. If something comes up, we'll let you know.
