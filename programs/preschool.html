Preschool Program

Preschool is where children's questions get bigger: Why is the sky blue? Where do worms go in winter? Can we build a bridge for the toy cars? Our Preschool Program takes those questions seriously and turns them into rich, hands-on learning that builds curiosity, kindness, and confidence.

Learning That Grows From Interest

Our preschool teachers watch and listen closely. When a group of children becomes fascinated by the construction site across the street, that might become a weeks-long study of machines, building, and measuring. Projects like these weave together reading, writing, math, science, and art in a way that feels like play, because it is.

What Children Explore

  • Early literacy: read-alouds, letter sounds, storytelling, and first attempts at writing names.
  • Math thinking: counting, sorting, patterns, shapes, and comparing.
  • Science: observing nature, simple experiments, and asking "what if?"
  • Creative arts: painting, collage, music, movement, and pretend play.
  • Physical growth: climbing, running, and fine-motor work like cutting and drawing.

Friendship and Community

Preschoolers are learning how to be part of a group. Through class meetings, shared jobs, and cooperative play, children practice listening, expressing ideas, working through disagreements, and caring for one another. These social and emotional skills are just as important as knowing letters and numbers.

Independence Every Day

Children serve themselves at meals, manage their belongings, and choose activities during free play. Teachers offer support while encouraging children to try first, which helps them see what they're capable of.

Keeping Families Close

We share what your child is learning, display their work, and make time to talk about progress. Families are always welcome to share traditions, skills, and stories with the class.
