Little Harbor Early Learning

Infant Program

The first year of life moves fast. One week your baby is studying the ceiling fan, and the next they're reaching for the ceiling fan. Our Infant Program gives families a calm, loving place where those early milestones are noticed, celebrated, and shared with you.

Where You'll Find It

The Infant Program is offered at six Little Harbor centers:

  • Evanston
  • Skokie
  • Rogers Park
  • Lincoln Park
  • Oak Park
  • Naperville

Following Your Baby's Rhythm

Babies don't run on a classroom clock, and we don't ask them to. Our infant teachers follow each child's own patterns for feeding, napping, and play. We talk with you about what happens at home so the day feels familiar. If your little one likes to be rocked a certain way or settles best with a favorite song, we want to know. Those small details make a big difference.

A Room Built for Discovery

Our infant rooms are bright, soft, and designed for exploring safely. You'll see low mirrors for tummy time, sturdy rails for first pull-ups, and baskets of textured toys, board books, and simple objects babies love to mouth, shake, and drop. Teachers get down on the floor, narrate what they see, and answer every coo and babble. That back-and-forth is the start of language.

What Our Teachers Focus On

  • Secure attachment: consistent caregivers who get to know your baby well.
  • Language: songs, rhymes, books, and lots of conversation.
  • Movement: space and encouragement for rolling, sitting, crawling, and cruising.
  • Senses: sounds, textures, and gentle sensory play.

Staying Connected

Leaving your baby with someone new is a big step. Teachers share how the day went at pickup, including meals, naps, and the funny or sweet moments in between. We welcome questions anytime and treat you as the expert on your child.

Come Visit

The best way to know if Little Harbor feels right is to see an infant room for yourself. Reach out to your nearest participating center to learn more and arrange a visit. We'd love to meet your family.
