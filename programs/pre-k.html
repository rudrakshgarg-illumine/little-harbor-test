Pre-K Program

The year before kindergarten is an exciting one. Children are ready for more challenge, more responsibility, and more independence. Our Pre-K Program prepares them for the next step with a thoughtful balance of play-based learning and growing structure, so they walk into kindergarten feeling capable and eager.

Where You'll Find It

The Pre-K Program is offered at six Little Harbor centers:

  • Evanston
  • Evanston West
  • Skokie
  • Lincoln Park
  • Oak Park
  • Naperville

Kindergarten Readiness, the Whole-Child Way

Being ready for kindergarten is about much more than reciting the alphabet. Our Pre-K teachers focus on the full set of skills children need to thrive in a classroom: focusing on a task, following multi-step directions, managing feelings, asking for help, and getting along with a group. Academic skills grow alongside these, never in place of them.

What Pre-K Children Work On

  • Literacy: letter sounds, rhyming, early reading, writing names and simple words, and retelling stories.
  • Math: counting with meaning, number recognition, simple addition and subtraction through games, measuring, and patterns.
  • Science and inquiry: making predictions, testing ideas, and recording what they notice.
  • Fine motor skills: pencil grip, cutting, and building with small materials.
  • Self-regulation: calming strategies, patience, and problem-solving with peers.

Longer Projects, Bigger Ideas

Pre-K children can stick with an idea for a long time. Classes dig into in-depth projects, whether designing a class store, studying how plants grow, or creating a book together. Children plan, revise, and present their work, building confidence in their own thinking.

Classroom Leaders

As the oldest children in the center, Pre-K students often take on leadership roles: helping with class jobs, modeling routines, and showing kindness to younger friends. They take real pride in it.

Partnering on the Transition

Teachers share your child's progress and are glad to talk through questions about the move to kindergarten, so your family feels prepared too.
