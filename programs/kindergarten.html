Kindergarten Program

Kindergarten is a milestone year, and it deserves a classroom where children are known, encouraged, and challenged. Our Kindergarten Program offers a warm, close-knit setting where young learners build strong academic foundations while still having the time and space to play, imagine, and grow.

Where You'll Find It

The Kindergarten Program is offered at one Little Harbor center:

  • Skokie

For many families at Skokie, this means children can continue with familiar teachers, friends, and routines rather than starting over somewhere new.

A Strong Academic Foundation

Kindergarten is when reading, writing, and math really start to click. Our teachers guide children through these skills with intention and care:

  • Reading: phonics, sight words, shared reading, and growing independence with books.
  • Writing: journaling, labeling drawings, and telling their own stories on paper.
  • Math: number sense, addition and subtraction, shapes, measurement, and data through hands-on materials.
  • Science and social studies: exploring the natural world, our community, and the people in it.

Still Room to Play

We believe five- and six-year-olds learn best when they're engaged, so play remains part of the day. Building, pretend play, art, music, and outdoor time give children chances to practice new skills, work together, and recharge. Learning feels joyful, not rushed.

Growing as a Classmate

Kindergarten is also about learning to be part of a classroom community. Children practice listening to others, sharing ideas in discussions, working in small groups, and taking responsibility for their work and belongings. Teachers help them build the confidence and resilience they'll carry into the grades ahead.

Individual Attention

Every child arrives with different strengths. Our teachers get to know each student well, notice where they shine and where they need support, and adjust their teaching to meet them there.

Working Together With Families

We keep families informed about what children are learning and how they're progressing, and we welcome conversations anytime. When home and school work together, children thrive.
