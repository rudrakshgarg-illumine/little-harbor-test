Twos Program

Two-year-olds are discovering that they are their own people, with their own ideas, preferences, and very strong opinions about which cup is the right cup. Our Twos Program meets children right where they are, offering structure that feels safe and freedom that feels exciting.

A Predictable Day

Twos thrive on routine. Our classrooms follow a steady rhythm of arrival, circle time, play, snack, outdoor time, meals, and rest. Picture schedules on the wall help children know what's coming next, which means fewer meltdowns and more confidence.

Play With Purpose

Every corner of a Twos classroom invites a different kind of learning:

  • Dramatic play: cooking pretend soup, caring for baby dolls, and acting out everyday life.
  • Art: painting, gluing, play dough, and plenty of mess.
  • Blocks and building: early math and problem-solving in action.
  • Books and music: stories, songs, and rhymes that build language every day.
  • Sensory tables: scooping, pouring, and exploring textures.

Growing Social Skills

This is the year children start playing with each other, not just beside each other. Teachers coach them through sharing, taking turns, and solving small conflicts with words. We help children name feelings like frustrated, proud, and excited, so they have the language to express what's going on inside.

Self-Help and Confidence

Twos love to help. They set out snack napkins, water plants, wipe tables, and put materials away. These small jobs build independence and a real sense of belonging. We also support toilet learning in partnership with families, following each child's readiness.

Staying in Touch

Teachers share the highlights of your child's day at pickup, and we're always glad to talk about new milestones or challenges at home.

Visit a Twos Classroom

With the Twos Program available at every Little Harbor center, there's likely one close to home or work. Reach out to the center nearest you to learn more and arrange a visit.
