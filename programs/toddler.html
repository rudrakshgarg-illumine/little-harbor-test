Toddler Program

Toddlers are scientists, explorers, and comedians all at once. They want to climb it, open it, dump it out, and do it "myself." Our Toddler Program welcomes all of that energy and turns it into joyful learning, with patient teachers who know that big feelings come with big growth.

Learning Through Doing

Toddlers learn with their whole bodies. Our classrooms are set up with low shelves, open materials, and plenty of room to move. Children pour water and sand, stack blocks until they crash, paint with brushes and fingers, and push wagons across the room. Teachers set up these experiences on purpose, then step back and let curiosity lead.

Words, Friends, and Feelings

This is a season of exploding vocabulary. Teachers name everything, read the same favorite books again and again, and sing songs with hand motions children can join. We also help toddlers take their first steps into friendship: noticing a friend who is sad, waiting a moment for a turn, and using words instead of grabbing. It doesn't happen overnight, and we're patient about that.

Building Independence

  • Washing hands and helping clean up
  • Trying to feed themselves at mealtimes
  • Putting on shoes and hanging up coats, with help
  • Making simple choices during play

When your family is ready for toilet learning, we'll work alongside you so the approach is consistent between home and school.

Outdoor Time

Fresh air matters. Toddlers spend time outside running, climbing, digging, and noticing bugs, leaves, and puddles. Many of our best lessons happen on the playground.

Partnering With You

Teachers share daily highlights at pickup and are always happy to talk through a tricky phase, from biting to bedtime battles. You're not doing this alone.

Come See a Toddler Room

Contact your nearest participating center to learn more and schedule a visit. Expect a little noise, a lot of laughter, and teachers who clearly love this age.
