Our Garden Beds Are Growing

June 6, 2022

Something exciting has been sprouting across Little Harbor Early Learning this spring. Several of our centers have been working with children to plant and care for outdoor garden beds, and the results have been nothing short of delightful.

Little hands, big jobs

Children helped from the very beginning. They scooped soil, pressed seeds into the dirt, and carefully patted everything down. Each class chose what to grow after reading books about gardens and looking at seed packets together.

Daily caretakers

Watering has become one of the most popular classroom jobs. Children check the beds during outdoor time, looking for new leaves and the occasional worm. One teacher told us her class now greets the garden every morning, as if it were another classmate.

Learning in every row

Gardening brings together so much of what we hope children will learn. They practice patience while waiting for seeds to sprout. They measure plant growth with blocks and string. They notice bugs, discuss weather, and draw what they see in nature journals. They also learn responsibility, because the plants depend on them.

Tasting the harvest

When the first herbs and greens were ready, children got to taste them. Some made faces, some asked for more, and nearly everyone was proud. Children are often more willing to try new foods when they grew them with their own hands.

How families can join in

You do not need a yard to garden with your child. A pot on a windowsill or balcony works beautifully. Try growing beans in a clear cup with a damp paper towel so your child can watch the roots form.

We are so proud of our young gardeners and grateful to the families who have shared seeds, tools, and encouragement. Stop by your child's classroom and ask them to tell you about their plants. They will have plenty to say.
