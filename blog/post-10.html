What We've Learned From Little Learners

January 15, 2026

A new year is a natural time to look back. As we think about the years since we first announced that Logan Square was opening soon on Milwaukee Avenue, and about all the children who have filled our classrooms across Little Harbor, we keep returning to one thought: the children have taught us as much as we have taught them.

Curiosity is everywhere

Children have shown us that a puddle, a pumpkin or a delivery truck can spark a week of learning. When we follow their questions, learning becomes deeper and more joyful.

Small moments matter most

We have seen how much a warm greeting at the door, a hand to hold on a hard day or a shared laugh at lunch can mean to a child. Big lessons often live inside small moments.

Kindness grows when it is noticed

From kindness trees to friends helping with zippers, we have watched children become more caring when their kind actions are seen and celebrated.

Families are our partners

Every note from home, every conversation at pickup and every family event has made our centers stronger. You know your children best, and we are grateful to learn alongside you.

Play is serious work

Block towers, mud kitchens, pretend bakeries and paint-covered hands are not breaks from learning. They are learning. We remain committed to giving children time and space to play.

As we begin this new year, we are excited for what is ahead: new friendships, new projects, new questions and plenty of new messes. We are also deeply thankful for the families, teachers and children who make each Little Harbor center feel like home.

Here is to another year of wonder, growth and muddy boots. We cannot wait to see what the children teach us next.
