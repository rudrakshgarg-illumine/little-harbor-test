Big News: A New Little Harbor Is on the Way

March 12, 2019

We have been waiting a long time to share this, and we are so happy we finally can: Logan Square is opening soon on Milwaukee Avenue.

For years, families have asked us whether Little Harbor might someday have a home in their neighborhood. Many of you have stopped us at pickup, sent notes home in backpacks, and told us about grandparents, cousins and neighbors who live nearby. We listened. Logan Square is a neighborhood full of families, murals, bike lanes, corner bakeries and parks, and it feels like exactly the kind of place where a Little Harbor center belongs.

Right now the space is a busy mix of sawdust, paint swatches and measuring tapes. Our team has spent many afternoons walking the rooms, imagining where the reading nooks will go, where the block corner will sprawl, and which windows will catch the best morning light for painting. We are paying close attention to the little things: hooks low enough for small hands, sinks children can reach on their own, and plenty of room for big, messy, joyful play.

What will not change is the heart of what we do. Like every Little Harbor center, Logan Square will be built around warm relationships, play-based learning and teachers who know each child well. We believe children learn best when they feel safe, seen and curious, and every decision we make in this new space comes back to that.

We know many of you will have questions, and we will share more details here on the blog as soon as they are confirmed. In the meantime, if you see us on Milwaukee Avenue carrying boxes of picture books, please say hello.

Thank you for being part of the Little Harbor family. We cannot wait to welcome even more neighbors into it.
