Winter Wonder: Indoor Activities for Cold Chicago Days

January 18, 2023

When the wind off the lake makes outdoor play tough, families need a few tricks up their sleeves. Here are some of our teachers' favorite indoor activities that keep little bodies moving and little minds busy.

Indoor Snow Play

Bring a bin of real snow inside and set it on a towel. Add cups, spoons, and small toys. Children can scoop, build, and watch it melt. Add a few drops of colored water for extra fun. It's sensory play and science in one.

Ice Excavation

Freeze small plastic toys in a container of water overnight. The next day, give your child warm water, droppers, and salt to "rescue" the treasures. They'll be busy, focused, and fascinated by how ice melts.

Blanket Fort Reading

Drape blankets over chairs, grab flashlights, and pile in with a stack of books. A cozy fort turns ordinary reading time into an adventure.

Movement Games

Burn off energy with games like:

  • Freeze dance: Dance to music and freeze when it stops.
  • Animal moves: Hop like a frog, waddle like a penguin, stomp like a bear.
  • Tape roads: Make lines on the floor with painter's tape to walk, balance, or drive toy cars along.
  • Pillow path: Lay pillows across the floor and hop from one to the next.

Paper Snowflakes

Fold paper and let your child practice snipping with child-safe scissors. Every snowflake is unique, just like the children making them. Hang them in a window for a wintry display.

Bake Together

Measuring, pouring, and stirring are wonderful early math and motor skills. Plus, the kitchen smells amazing.

In our classrooms this season, children are learning about animals that hibernate, comparing snowflakes, and making plenty of "hot cocoa" in the dramatic play area. Bundle up, stay warm, and enjoy the cozy moments winter brings.
