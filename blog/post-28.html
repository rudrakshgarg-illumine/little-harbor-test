Celebrating Our Teachers: The Heart of Little Harbor

May 8, 2024

Every day, across all ten Little Harbor centers, our teachers do something remarkable. They tie shoes and wipe noses, yes, but they also notice the quiet child who needs a little encouragement, celebrate the first time a toddler says a new word, and turn ordinary moments into real learning.

This week, we want to take a moment to say thank you.

What Families Shared

We asked families to tell us what they appreciate about their child's teachers, and the notes poured in. A few themes came up again and again:

  • Patience: "She never seems rushed, even on the hardest days."
  • Communication: "I always know how my son's day went."
  • Warmth: "My daughter runs in every morning to give her teacher a hug."
  • Creativity: "The projects they come up with amaze me."

We shared every note with our teams, and there may have been a few happy tears in the break rooms.

Behind the Scenes

What families don't always see is the work that happens behind the scenes. Our teachers plan activities, observe and document each child's growth, prepare classrooms, and continue learning themselves. They care deeply about getting it right for every child.

Ways to Show Appreciation

Thank-you gestures don't need to be big. Some of the most treasured are:

  • A handwritten note from you
  • A drawing made by your child
  • A kind word at pickup
  • Sharing a specific moment your child talked about at home

Our Thanks

To every teacher, assistant, and staff member at Little Harbor: thank you for the care, joy, and dedication you bring to our children every single day. You make our centers feel like a second home.

And to our families: thank you for trusting us with your children. It is a privilege we never take lightly.
