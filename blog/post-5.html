Hello, Logan Square Neighbors

August 18, 2021

It is hard to believe how much has changed since we first shared that Logan Square was opening soon on Milwaukee Avenue. Today, the center is full of the sounds we love most: children laughing, songs at circle time, blocks tumbling and teachers asking, "What do you notice?"

We wanted to take a moment to thank the Logan Square families who have trusted us with their children. Every new center begins with a group of families who are willing to try something new, and you have shaped this community in ways we could not have planned.

Some of our favorite moments so far:

  • The morning a group of children spent nearly an hour watching a delivery truck unload outside the window and then built their own truck from cardboard boxes.
  • Neighborhood walks where children counted bicycles, pointed out murals and waved at friendly dogs.
  • The first family picnic, where parents swapped stories and children shared snacks they swore they did not like at home.
  • A dramatic play corner that became a bakery, then a vet clinic, then a rocket ship, all in one week.

Being part of Milwaukee Avenue has been a gift. The neighborhood's creativity, energy and love of local places show up in our classrooms every day. Children bring stories about the park, the library and their favorite corner shops, and teachers weave those stories into projects and conversations.

As Logan Square continues to grow, our focus stays the same: warm relationships, play-based learning and a place where every child feels at home. We are proud to be your neighbors, and we look forward to many more years of muddy boots, finger paintings and first friendships.

If you pass by and see little faces pressed against the window, give them a wave. They will almost certainly wave back.
