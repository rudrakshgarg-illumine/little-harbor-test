Summer Splash Days and Sidewalk Science

July 10, 2026

Summer at Little Harbor is bright, busy, and just a little bit wet. When the sun comes out, our classrooms move outdoors as often as possible, and the learning comes right along with us.

Splash Day Discoveries

Water play is a summer favorite. With buckets, cups, funnels, and sponges, children experiment with pouring, filling, and squeezing. Teachers ask questions like "Which cup holds more?" and "What happens if we pour faster?" It looks like pure fun, and it is, but it's also early science and math.

Sink or Float?

One favorite activity is the classic sink-or-float test. Children gather objects like leaves, rocks, corks, and toy boats, then make predictions before dropping each one into the water. There are always a few surprises, and lots of cheering when a guess turns out right.

Sidewalk Science

Chalk comes out in full force this time of year. Beyond drawing, children have been:

  • Tracing shadows at different times of day to see how they move
  • Painting with water and watching it disappear in the sun
  • Making chalk paint by mixing crushed chalk and water
  • Drawing hopscotch grids for counting and jumping practice

Bug Watching

Magnifying glasses in hand, our young scientists have been tracking ants, butterflies, and ladybugs. Several classes have started "bug journals" filled with drawings and observations.

Staying Safe in the Sun

Outdoor fun goes hand in hand with care. Our teachers make sure children take shade breaks and stay hydrated throughout the day. Please send your child in sun-ready clothing and label hats and water bottles so everything finds its way home.

Try It at Home

A bucket of water, a few kitchen utensils, and a sunny afternoon are all you need. Let your child lead, ask questions, and get a little messy.

We hope your summer is full of sunshine, splashes, and wonder. We'll see you outside!
