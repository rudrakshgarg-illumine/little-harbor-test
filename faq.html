Little Harbor Early Learning

Frequently Asked Questions

These answers apply to all 10 Little Harbor centers. If you have a question that is not covered here, call our main number at (847) 555-0100 or contact your center directly.

What age groups do you care for?

Our classrooms serve infants, toddlers, twos and preschoolers, each in a space designed for their stage of development.

What are your teacher-to-child ratios?

Infants 1:4, toddlers 1:5, twos 1:6, preschool 1:10.

Are meals included?

Breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack are included; menus are nut-free.

My child has a nut allergy. Is Little Harbor a safe choice?

Our menus are nut-free at every center. Please share any allergy or dietary needs with your center so teachers can plan for your child.

When should my child stay home sick?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to their classroom. This protects your child, their classmates and our teachers.

How will I know how my child's day went?

Parents get daily photos and reports in the Little Harbor app.

Is there parking at the centers?

Free parking at every center except Lincoln Park, which has 15-minute drop-off spots only.

What days are the centers closed?

All centers close for New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the Friday after, December 24 and 25.

Where are your centers located?

We have 10 centers: Evanston, Evanston West, Skokie, Wilmette, Rogers Park, Lincoln Park, Logan Square, Oak Park, Naperville and Aurora. Full addresses and phone numbers are on our contact page.

How can I schedule a tour?

Call the center you are interested in, or reach our main number at (847) 555-0100, and our team will help you arrange a visit.

How long has Little Harbor been caring for children?

Little Harbor Early Learning has been caring for children and families since 2009.

Who do I contact about enrollment, availability or tuition?

Each center can answer questions about its own classrooms and openings. Contact your nearest center directly, or call (847) 555-0100 and we will connect you.
