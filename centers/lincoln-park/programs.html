Little Harbor Early Learning

Programs at Lincoln Park

At Little Harbor Lincoln Park, we care for children ages 6 weeks to 5 years across five programs. Each classroom is shaped around what children at that stage need most, from cuddles and steady routines for our youngest to the confidence and curiosity our oldest carry into kindergarten. In every program, children hear both English and Mandarin. Mandarin enrichment is part of every day, and our rooftop playground gives children a chance to move and explore outdoors.

Infant

Our Infant program welcomes babies as young as 6 weeks. In this room, everything begins with trust. Teachers follow each baby's own rhythms for feeding, napping and playtime. They talk, sing and read throughout the day, often in both English and Mandarin, so babies are surrounded by warm voices and rich language from the very start. We keep families closely connected and share the small moments that make up a baby's day.

Toddler

Toddlers are on the move, and our Toddler classroom is built for that. Children explore with their hands and bodies, practice new words and begin to play alongside friends. Teachers support growing independence, like trying a spoon, washing hands or helping clean up. They also offer plenty of comfort when big feelings arrive. Simple Mandarin songs and phrases become familiar parts of the daily routine.

Twos

Two-year-olds are full of opinions, questions and energy. In our Twos program, children build language, try early pretend play and learn to share space and materials with classmates. Teachers guide them through routines that build confidence and self-help skills. Daily Mandarin enrichment keeps growing their ear for a second language through music, movement and stories.

Preschool

In Preschool, children dive into longer projects, richer conversations and more cooperative play. They explore early literacy and math through hands-on activities, art, building and dramatic play. Teachers encourage children to ask questions, solve problems and express their ideas. Mandarin becomes more interactive here, as children use words and phrases in games, songs and conversation.

Pre-K

Our Pre-K program is the bridge to kindergarten. Children strengthen the skills that help them thrive in a classroom: listening, taking turns, following multi-step directions and working with friends. They also grow early reading, writing and number skills. They carry their years of daily Mandarin enrichment with them, along with a sense of pride in what they can do. We work with families to make the move to kindergarten feel exciting rather than overwhelming.
