Our Team at Lincoln Park

A great early learning center comes down to its people. At Little Harbor Lincoln Park, our team cares for up to 110 children, ages 6 weeks to 5 years. We are proud of the relationships we build with children and families every day.

Meet Our Director: Olivia Chen

Olivia Chen leads our Lincoln Park center. She sets the tone for the whole building: warm, organized and focused on what each child needs to feel safe and keep growing. Families often find her in the hallways at drop-off, in classrooms throughout the day and on tours, where she gets to know new families and answers their questions.

Olivia helps families through every stage, from a baby's first day in our Infant room to the last weeks of Pre-K before kindergarten. If you have a question, a concern or something to celebrate, her door is open. You can reach the center at (773) 555-0126.

Our Teachers

Our teachers are the heart of the Fullerton center. They are the people who know just how your toddler likes to be rocked before nap, which song gets your two-year-old giggling, and which friend your preschooler can't wait to see each morning.

The teachers you'll meet here are:

  • Bilingual-minded. Our classrooms use English and Mandarin. Teachers bring Mandarin enrichment into every day through songs, stories, greetings and play, so a second language feels like a natural part of childhood.
  • Patient and attentive. They follow children's cues, notice small changes and give children time to try, struggle a little and succeed.
  • Playful. Our teachers know that children learn through play. That includes building, painting and pretending, as well as running around on our rooftop playground.
  • Partners with families. Teachers share what your child did, said and discovered. They want to hear from you, too, because you know your child best.
