Little Harbor Early Learning – Lincoln Park

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning in Lincoln Park, known to many of our families simply as "Fullerton." Since opening our doors in 2011, we have been a warm, familiar stop on the morning routine for neighborhood families, a place where little ones are known by name and every day holds something to discover.

Our center cares for up to 110 children, ages 6 weeks to 5 years. That range lets many families stay with us from a baby's first months through the year before kindergarten. Children grow up alongside friends they have known since they were toddling, and parents get to know one another along the way.

What Makes Lincoln Park Special

  • Mandarin enrichment daily. Our classrooms use both English and Mandarin. Every day, children hear and use Mandarin through songs, stories, greetings and play. Their second language becomes a natural part of the day.
  • A rooftop playground. In the middle of the city, our children have their own outdoor space up top. It's a place to run, climb and enjoy fresh air with a view of the neighborhood.
  • A neighborhood feel. Many families walk, bike or take transit to reach us. Our location on Fullerton Avenue fits right into city life.

Programs

We offer five programs, each designed around the needs of children at that stage: Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool and Pre-K. Visit our Programs page to learn more about each one.

A Note About Parking

Most Little Harbor centers have free parking, but Lincoln Park does not. We have 15-minute drop-off spots only. They work well for a quick hello-and-goodbye at drop-off and a smooth pickup. Plan for a brief stop, or consider walking or taking transit if that suits your family.

Come See Us

The best way to know if Lincoln Park is the right fit is to visit. We offer tours Mon and Wed 10:30 AM, and each tour lasts 30 minutes. Our director, Olivia Chen, and our team would love to show you around, introduce you to our classrooms and rooftop, and answer your questions. Call us at (773) 555-0126 to plan your visit.
