Tour Little Harbor Lincoln Park

Choosing care for your child is a big decision, and nothing helps quite like seeing a center in person. We'd love to welcome you to our Lincoln Park center at 845 W Fullerton Avenue, Chicago, IL 60614.

Tour Times

Mon and Wed 10:30 AM

Each tour lasts 30 minutes. To reserve your spot, call us at (773) 555-0126.

What to Expect

  • A walk through our classrooms. See our Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool and Pre-K rooms, and get a feel for a real day at our center.
  • Mandarin in action. Our classrooms use English and Mandarin, and Mandarin enrichment happens daily. You may hear children singing or chatting in both languages.
  • Our rooftop playground. See the outdoor space where children run, climb and play above the city.
  • Time for your questions. Our director, Olivia Chen, is glad to talk through anything on your mind, from routines to our programs for children ages 6 weeks to 5 years.

Before You Come

Please note that Lincoln Park does not have free parking. We have 15-minute drop-off spots only. Since tours last 30 minutes, you may want to walk, take transit or plan for nearby street parking.

Little ones are welcome to come along. We look forward to meeting you!
