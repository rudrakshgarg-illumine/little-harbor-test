Little Harbor Early Learning

Frequently Asked Questions – Lincoln Park

What ages do you serve?

We care for children ages 6 weeks to 5 years. Our programs are Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool and Pre-K.

What are your hours?

We are open Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:30 PM.

Where are you located?

We are at 845 W Fullerton Avenue, Chicago, IL 60614. Many families know us as the "Fullerton" center.

Is there parking?

Lincoln Park is the one Little Harbor center without free parking. We have 15-minute drop-off spots only, so plan for a quick stop at drop-off and pickup.

Do children learn Mandarin?

Yes. Our classrooms use English and Mandarin, and Mandarin enrichment is part of every day for children in all of our programs.

Do the children go outside?

Yes. Our center has a rooftop playground, which gives children a safe outdoor space to play right here in the city.

How many children attend the center?

Our center has a capacity of 110 children.

When can my child return after being sick?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center.

Who is the director?

Our director is Olivia Chen. She is happy to talk with families about any questions.

How can I schedule a tour?

Tours are offered Mon and Wed at 10:30 AM and last 30 minutes. Call us at (773) 555-0126 to reserve a time.
