Little Harbor Early Learning

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages do you serve?

We care for children from 6 weeks to 5 years, through our Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K programs.

What are your hours?

Our Evanston center is open Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

Where are you located?

We are at 2140 Central Street, Evanston, IL 60201. Many families know us as our Central Street center.

Is parking available?

Yes. We offer free parking for families at our Evanston center.

Do you offer a Spanish program?

Yes. Our Evanston center has a Spanish immersion classroom for twos and preschool, where children hear and use Spanish throughout their day.

What languages does your staff speak?

Our team communicates with families in English and Spanish.

Who is the director?

Maria Delgado is the director of our Evanston center. You can reach her by calling (847) 555-0121.

How many children attend your center?

Our Evanston center has a capacity of 120 children. We have been serving families on Central Street since 2009.

When can my child return after being sick?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center. Full details are available in the sick policy on our website.

How can I schedule a tour?

Tours are offered on Tue and Thu 10:00 AM and last 30 minutes. Call us at (847) 555-0121 to plan your visit.
