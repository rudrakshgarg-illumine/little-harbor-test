Little Harbor Early Learning – Evanston

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning in Evanston, known to many local families simply as our Central Street center. Since opening our doors in 2009, we have been a place where babies take their first wobbly steps, toddlers discover the joy of new words, and preschoolers grow into confident, curious kids ready for what comes next. With room for 120 children, our center is big enough to offer a full range of programs and small enough that familiar faces greet you every morning.

A Place to Grow, Right on Central Street

We care for children from 6 weeks to 5 years, which means many of our families stay with us from infancy all the way through Pre-K. Our classrooms follow children as they grow: Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K. Each step builds on the one before, so children move forward in a setting they already know and trust.

What Makes Evanston Special

Our Evanston center is home to a Spanish immersion classroom for twos and preschool. In this classroom, children spend their day hearing and using Spanish through songs, stories, play, and everyday routines. Young children absorb language naturally, and immersion lets them do just that, in a warm and playful environment. Our team communicates with families in both English and Spanish, so whichever language feels most like home to you, we are glad to talk with you in it.
