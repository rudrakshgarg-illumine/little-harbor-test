Tour Little Harbor Evanston

Choosing a place for your child is one of the biggest decisions you will make, and we believe nothing replaces seeing a center with your own eyes. We warmly invite you to visit our Central Street center and experience a day in the life at Little Harbor Evanston.

Tour Times

We offer tours on Tue and Thu 10:00 AM. Each tour lasts 30 minutes, making it easy to fit into a busy morning.

What to Expect

  • A walk through our classrooms, from Infant and Toddler to Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K
  • A look at our Spanish immersion classroom for twos and preschool
  • A chance to see our teachers and children in action
  • Time to ask questions about our programs, daily routines, and policies

During your visit, you will get a feel for the rhythm of our day and the warmth of our team. We are happy to talk with you in English or Spanish, whichever is most comfortable for your family.

Getting Here

You will find us at 2140 Central Street, Evanston, IL 60201. Free parking is available, so you can head right inside when you arrive.

Schedule Your Visit

To plan your tour, call us at (847) 555-0121. Bring your questions, bring your little one if you like, and come see why Evanston families have trusted Little Harbor since 2009. We look forward to welcoming you.
