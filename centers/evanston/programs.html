Programs at Little Harbor Evanston

At our Central Street center, we offer five programs that support children from 6 weeks to 5 years. Each classroom is designed around what children need most at that stage of life, and each one prepares them gently for the next. Our team speaks English and Spanish, and our Spanish immersion classroom for twos and preschool is one of the things families love most about our Evanston center.

Infant

Our Infant program welcomes babies starting at 6 weeks old. In this calm, nurturing room, our teachers follow each baby's own rhythm for feeding, napping, and play. We know how big it feels to leave your baby in someone else's care, so we focus on building trust, both with your little one and with you. Through cuddles, songs, tummy time, and lots of face-to-face conversation, babies begin to explore the world from a place of security.

Toddler

Toddlers are on the move, and our Toddler classroom is built for exploring. Children practice new skills every day: walking, climbing, stacking, pouring, and, of course, talking. Teachers guide toddlers through simple routines that help them feel capable and confident, while supporting the early social skills that come with playing alongside friends.

Twos

Two-year-olds are bursting with language, independence, and big feelings. Our Twos program gives them room to grow in all three. Children build vocabulary through stories and songs, try out new self-help skills, and learn to name and manage their emotions with patient support from teachers. Families interested in a second language can also explore our Spanish immersion classroom for twos.

Preschool

In Preschool, children dive into hands-on learning through art, building, pretend play, early math, and early literacy. They learn to share ideas, solve problems together, and follow their curiosity wherever it leads. Our Spanish immersion classroom for preschool offers children a chance to spend their day learning and playing in Spanish, absorbing a new language the natural way.

Pre-K

Our Pre-K program helps children take the final steps before kindergarten. Children strengthen early reading and math skills, practice listening and following multi-step directions, and build the independence and friendships that make the move to elementary school feel exciting rather than overwhelming. By the end of Pre-K, our oldest students are proud, ready learners.

Spanish Immersion

Our Spanish immersion classroom for twos and preschool is a special part of life at Little Harbor Evanston. Children hear and use Spanish throughout their day, in songs, stories, play, and routines. It is a joyful, natural way for young children to begin a second language, whether Spanish is new to your family or already spoken at home.
