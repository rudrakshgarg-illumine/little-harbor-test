Little Harbor Early Learning

Meet Our Evanston Team

A great early learning center is really about people. At Little Harbor Evanston, our team is the reason families trust us with their children every weekday, Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM. Since 2009, our Central Street center has been a place where caring adults help children feel safe, known, and excited to learn.

Our Director: Maria Delgado

Maria Delgado leads our Evanston center with warmth and steady attention to every child and family in our community. With room for 120 children across our Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K programs, there is a lot happening in our building each day, and Maria makes sure each family still feels personally welcomed. She is the person to talk to when you have questions about enrollment, want to share something about your child, or simply want to know how things are going. Parents can reach her through our front desk at (847) 555-0121.

Maria also supports our team in keeping communication open with families in both English and Spanish, so that every parent can feel comfortable and fully informed.

Our Teachers

The teachers at Little Harbor Evanston are the kind of people children run to at drop-off. They get down on the floor to play, notice the small milestones, and celebrate them with you at pickup. They know that a baby's first smile, a toddler's new word, and a preschooler's first written name are all big moments.

Our teachers bring patience, creativity, and genuine affection to their classrooms. They build daily routines that help children feel secure, and they plan activities that spark curiosity and joy. In our Spanish immersion classroom for twos and preschool, teachers guide children through their day in Spanish, making a new language feel natural and fun.

Above all, our teachers see themselves as partners with families. They share updates, listen to your insights about your child, and work alongside you to help your little one thrive.
