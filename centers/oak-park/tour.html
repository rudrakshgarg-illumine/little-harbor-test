Tour Little Harbor Oak Park

Choosing child care is a big decision, and nothing helps more than seeing a center in person. We warmly invite you to visit our Lake Street center, meet our team, and picture your child's day with us.

Tour Times

Tours are offered Wed and Fri at 9:00 AM. Each tour lasts about 30 minutes, so it fits easily into a busy morning.

What to Expect

  • A look inside our classrooms. You will see our Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K rooms and get a feel for how each one supports children at that stage.
  • Time to ask questions. Bring your list. We are happy to talk about daily routines, our sick policy, our monthly staff training days, and anything else on your mind.
  • A chance to meet our team. Our director, Rachel Kim, and our teachers love meeting new families.

Getting Here

We are located at 1036 Lake Street, Oak Park, IL 60301. Free parking is available, so you can arrive without worrying about finding a spot.

Please keep in mind that we are closed the first Monday of every month for staff training.

Schedule Your Visit

Call us at (708) 555-0128 to plan your tour. We look forward to welcoming you to Lake Street.
