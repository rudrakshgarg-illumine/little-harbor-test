Little Harbor Early Learning – Oak Park

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning in Oak Park, known to many of our families simply as "Nine Mile." Since opening our doors in 2014, this center has been a place where little ones are greeted by name, where first steps and first friendships happen, and where parents can head off to work knowing their child is in caring, attentive hands.

Our Oak Park center serves children from 6 weeks to 5 years, with room for up to 100 children. That size lets us offer a full range of age-based classrooms while still feeling like a neighborhood school. Teachers know the children down the hall, older children wave to the babies they once shared a building with, and families get to know one another at pickup.

Hours

We are open Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM, giving working families a full day of coverage with time to spare on both ends of the commute.

Please note: our center is closed the first Monday of every month for staff training. We take this time seriously. It is when our team comes together to learn, plan, and grow, so they can bring fresh ideas and renewed energy back to your child's classroom. We recommend marking these dates on your family calendar.

Ages and Programs

From the earliest weeks of infancy through the year before kindergarten, children can grow with us through five programs:

  • Infant
  • Toddler
  • Twos
  • Preschool
  • Pre-K

Each classroom is designed around what children at that stage need most, whether that is a calm, responsive caregiver, room to explore, or the confidence and skills that set them up for kindergarten.

What Makes Nine Mile Special

Families tell us what they love most is the consistency. Many of our children start with us as infants and stay through Pre-K, which means they grow up alongside familiar faces in a familiar place. Under the leadership of our director, Rachel Kim, our team works to make every classroom feel warm, organized, and joyful.

Our monthly staff training days are another part of what sets us apart. Rather than squeezing professional development into rushed moments, we set aside dedicated time each month so our teachers can keep learning together.

The best way to understand Nine Mile is to see it for yourself. We offer tours on Wed and Fri at 9:00 AM, and each tour lasts about 30 minutes. We would love to meet you and your child.
