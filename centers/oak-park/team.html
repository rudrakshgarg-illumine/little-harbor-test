Meet Our Oak Park Team

Great early learning comes down to people. At Little Harbor Oak Park, our team is the heart of everything we do, and families often tell us that the warmth of our teachers is what they notice first.

Our Director: Rachel Kim

Rachel Kim leads our Nine Mile center with a steady hand and an open door. As director, Rachel oversees every classroom, supports our teachers, and serves as a trusted partner to the families we care for. She knows that choosing child care is one of the most important decisions a family makes, and she is happy to talk through questions, listen to concerns, and help each child and family settle in.

Rachel believes that when teachers feel supported, children thrive. That belief guides how she leads, from the day-to-day rhythm of the center to the dedicated training days we hold on the first Monday of every month.

Our Teachers

Our teachers are the people who greet your child each morning, comfort them when they need it, and cheer them on as they try new things. They are patient, playful, and genuinely interested in the children in their care.

Here is what you can expect from our teaching team:

  • Warm relationships. Teachers take time to know each child's personality, preferences, and needs.
  • Clear communication. Teachers keep families informed about their child's day and growth.
  • Ongoing learning. Our center closes the first Monday of every month for staff training, so teachers can keep sharpening their skills together.
  • Teamwork. Teachers across our Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K classrooms work together, helping children move smoothly from one stage to the next.
