Little Harbor Early Learning

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages do you serve?

Our Oak Park center serves children from 6 weeks to 5 years.

What programs do you offer?

We offer five programs: Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K.

What are your hours?

We are open Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

Are there any days the center is closed?

Yes. We are closed the first Monday of every month for staff training. We recommend adding these dates to your family calendar so you can plan ahead.

Where are you located?

We are at 1036 Lake Street, Oak Park, IL 60301. Many families know us as our "Lake Street" center.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available at our Oak Park center.

When can I tour the center?

Tours are offered Wed and Fri at 9:00 AM. Each tour lasts about 30 minutes.

What is your sick policy?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center. This helps keep all of our children and teachers healthy.

What languages are spoken in the classroom?

Our Oak Park classrooms are taught in English.

How can I reach the center?

Call us at (708) 555-0128. Our director, Rachel Kim, and our team are happy to answer your questions.
