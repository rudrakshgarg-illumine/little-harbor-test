Little Harbor Early Learning

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning – Aurora

Little Harbor Early Learning in Aurora opened its doors in 2023, and from the very first morning our goal has been simple: to be a warm, steady, joyful place where young children feel known and families feel at home. We are a small center with a capacity of 50 children, which means faces become familiar quickly. Teachers know which child likes to be greeted with a high five and which one needs a quiet minute before joining the group.

We are part of Little Harbor Early Learning, a group of 10 early learning centers near Chicago. Our Aurora center brings that shared commitment to caring, play-filled early childhood into a close-knit community setting.

Who We Serve

We care for children ages 2 to 5 years through two programs: Twos and Preschool. Whether your child is just discovering words and friendships or getting ready for the next big step, there is a classroom here designed for where they are right now.

Hours

We are open Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM, so working families can count on a consistent, predictable day.

What Makes Aurora Special

A real person answers your call. Our center uses a staff-only phone line, and we do not use an AI assistant. When you call (630) 555-0130, you are reaching the people who actually care for children here. We think that matters, especially when you have a question about your own child.

English and Spanish. Our center welcomes families in both English and Spanish. We want every parent and grandparent to feel comfortable asking questions, sharing news from home, and being part of their child's day.

A small, personal community. With room for 50 children, our center stays intimate. Children build real friendships, families get to know one another at pickup, and our team gets to know each child as an individual.

Thoughtful leadership. Our center is led by Director Marcus Bell, who is here to welcome families, answer questions, and help every child settle in well.
