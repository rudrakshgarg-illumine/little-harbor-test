Visit Little Harbor Aurora

Choosing an early learning center is a big decision, and nothing replaces seeing it for yourself. We warmly invite you to tour Little Harbor Early Learning – Aurora and get a feel for our classrooms, our team, and the everyday rhythm of our center.

Tour Times

Tours are offered on Mon 10:00 AM. Each tour lasts 30 minutes, so it fits easily into a busy week.

To plan your visit, call us at (303) 555-0130. Our staff-only phone line means you will speak directly with our team, never an AI assistant.

What to Expect

  • A warm welcome. You may be greeted by our director, Marcus Bell, who can share how our center works and answer your questions.
  • A look inside our classrooms. See where children in our Twos and Preschool programs spend their days.
  • Time for your questions. Ask about daily routines, our hours (Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM), our sick policy, or anything else on your mind.
  • Conversation in English or Spanish. Our center welcomes families in both languages.

Getting Here

We are located at 2200 S Havana Street, Aurora, CO 80014. Free parking is available at our center, so arriving for your tour is easy.

We serve children ages 2 to 5 years, and we would love to meet your family. See you on Monday!
