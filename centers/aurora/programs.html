Our Programs

At Little Harbor Early Learning – Aurora, we serve children ages 2 to 5 years through two programs: Twos and Preschool. Both programs run during our center hours, Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM, and both are built on the same belief: young children learn best when they feel safe, cared for, and free to explore.

Twos

Two-year-olds are busy people. They are finding new words every week, testing what their bodies can do, and figuring out how to be around other children. Our Twos program is designed to meet them right in that exciting, sometimes wobbly stage. The day is shaped by gentle routines that help children know what comes next, from arrival to snack to outdoor time to rest, so they can relax and enjoy the moment.

In the Twos classroom, play is the work. Children stack and knock down, pour and scoop, paint with big brushes, listen to stories, and sing songs together. Teachers narrate what children are doing, give names to feelings, and celebrate small victories like putting on a jacket or sharing a toy for the first time. We know this is often a child's first experience away from home, so we take time to build trust with both children and families.

Because our center welcomes families in English and Spanish, children may hear songs, greetings, and everyday words in both languages, and parents can share how their child is doing in the language that feels most natural.

Preschool

Our Preschool program serves older children within our 2 to 5 years age range as they grow more curious, more social, and more independent. Preschoolers want to know how things work, why things happen, and what their friends think. Our classrooms give them room to ask those questions and find their own answers.

Preschool days balance group time with plenty of choice. Children might build a city out of blocks, look closely at leaves, act out a story in the dramatic play area, draw and write their names, or count the steps out to the playground. Teachers guide conversations, encourage problem solving, and help children practice the social skills that matter so much: taking turns, working through disagreements, and caring for one another.

As children get closer to the next big step, Preschool helps them build confidence in following routines, expressing their needs, and trying new things. Our goal is for children to leave us feeling capable and excited to learn.
