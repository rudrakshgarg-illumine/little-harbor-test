Little Harbor Early Learning

Our Team

A school is only as good as the people in it. At Little Harbor Early Learning – Aurora, our team is the heart of everything we do. Since opening in 2023, we have worked to build a group of caring adults who show up every day ready to listen, play, comfort, and teach.

Meet Our Director: Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell leads our Aurora center. As director, Marcus is the person who helps hold our community together. He welcomes new families, supports our teachers, and makes sure the day runs smoothly from the moment doors open at 7:00 AM to pickup at 6:00 PM.

Marcus believes families should always feel informed and heard. If you have a question about enrollment, a concern about your child, or just want to share a funny story from home, he wants to hear it. He is often the first face you will meet on a tour, and he takes pride in knowing the children and families who make our center what it is.

Our Teachers

Our teachers care for children ages 2 to 5 years in our Twos and Preschool programs. They are the kind of people who get down on the floor to build a tower, notice when a child is having a hard morning, and remember that your daughter just became a big sister.

What our teachers share:

  • Patience and warmth. Young children are learning how to handle big feelings. Our teachers respond calmly and kindly, helping children feel safe.
  • Respect for play. They understand that play is how young children learn, and they plan classrooms full of things to explore.
  • Partnership with families. They share what your child did, what they enjoyed, and what they are working on.
  • Welcoming every family. Our center serves families in English and Spanish, so communication feels comfortable for everyone.

A Small Center, A Close Team

With a capacity of 50 children, our center is small enough that every adult in the building gets to know the children. That closeness makes a difference for children who are just learning to trust new grown-ups.
