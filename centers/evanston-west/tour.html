Tour Evanston West

Choosing an early learning center is a big decision, and nothing helps more than seeing a place for yourself. We warmly invite you to visit Little Harbor's Evanston West center, known to many families as Dodge Avenue.

  • Where: 1820 Dodge Avenue, Evanston, IL 60201
  • Parking: Free parking
  • To schedule: Call (847) 555-0122

What to Expect

Your tour lasts 30 minutes and gives you a real look at a morning at Evanston West. You'll walk through our classrooms for Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K and see how teachers and children spend their time together. Because tours happen mid-morning, you'll likely catch children in the middle of play, projects, and conversations.

We'll also show you our outdoor nature classroom, the space that makes our center special. It's a great chance to picture your child exploring, digging, and discovering outside.

Along the way, you can ask about our programs, daily routines, and anything else on your mind. It's helpful to know before you arrive that we serve children ages 2 to 5 years and do not offer infant care, and that our hours are Mon-Fri 7:30 AM - 6:00 PM.

We Look Forward to Meeting You

Director Kevin Osei and our team are happy to welcome you. Give us a call at (847) 555-0122 to reserve a Wednesday tour. We can't wait to show you around.
