Little Harbor Early Learning

Programs at Evanston West

At Little Harbor's Evanston West center on Dodge Avenue, we serve children ages 2 to 5 years through three programs: Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K. Each program meets children where they are and gently guides them toward what comes next. Across every classroom, our outdoor nature classroom is woven into the day, giving children room to move, explore, and learn from the world around them. All programs run within our center hours of Mon-Fri 7:30 AM - 6:00 PM, and instruction is in English.

Twos

Two-year-olds are busy people. They are discovering independence, testing words, and learning that other children can be wonderful (and sometimes frustrating) playmates. Our Twos program is built around warm routines, plenty of hands-on play, and lots of patience. Teachers help children practice simple self-help skills, name their feelings, and take turns. Sensory play, songs, stories, and time in the outdoor nature classroom give our youngest learners a chance to dig, pour, splash, and wonder. Most of all, we want twos to feel safe, loved, and excited to come back tomorrow.

Preschool

In Preschool, children's curiosity takes off. They ask "why?" about everything, and our teachers love helping them find out. The Preschool program balances child-led play with small-group activities that build early literacy, math thinking, and social skills. Children might sort pinecones by size, retell a favorite story, or work together to build something tall. Outdoors, they observe changes in plants and weather, collect natural treasures, and use their whole bodies to run, climb, and balance. Preschoolers also grow in friendship skills: listening, sharing ideas, and working through disagreements with words.

Pre-K

Pre-K is the year children start looking toward kindergarten, and our program helps them get there with confidence. Pre-K classrooms build on everything children have learned so far, with more focus on letters and sounds, counting and patterns, following multi-step directions, and staying with a task. Just as important, children practice the social and emotional skills that make the move to school smoother: independence, self-regulation, and kindness toward classmates. The outdoor nature classroom becomes a place for real investigation, where children make predictions, notice details, and talk about what they discover.

Learning Outdoors, Every Step of the Way

Our outdoor nature classroom is what makes Evanston West special. Whether a child is two or five, time outside supports focus, physical health, and a sense of wonder. It also gives children a chance to care for something bigger than themselves.
