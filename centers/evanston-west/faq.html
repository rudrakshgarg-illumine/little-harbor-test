Frequently Asked Questions – Evanston West

Where is Evanston West located?

We are at 1820 Dodge Avenue, Evanston, IL 60201. Many families call our center "Dodge Avenue."

What are your hours?

We are open Mon-Fri 7:30 AM - 6:00 PM.

What ages do you serve?

Evanston West serves children ages 2 to 5 years through our Twos, Preschool, and Pre-K programs.

Do you offer infant care?

No. Evanston West does not offer infant care. Our center focuses on children ages 2 to 5 years.

What makes Evanston West different?

Our outdoor nature classroom is a big part of daily life here. Children spend time exploring, observing, and learning from the natural world.

Who is the director?

Our director is Kevin Osei. You can reach the center at (847) 555-0122.

What language are classes taught in?

Instruction at Evanston West is in English.

Is there parking?

Yes. Evanston West has free parking for drop-off, pick-up, and tours.

How big is the center?

Evanston West opened in 2021 and has a capacity of 60 children.

What is your sick policy?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center.
