Little Harbor Early Learning – Evanston West

Welcome to Evanston West, the Little Harbor center many families simply call Dodge Avenue. Since opening our doors in 2021, we have been a warm, close-knit place where children ages 2 to 5 years come to play, explore, ask big questions, and grow into confident learners. With a capacity of 60 children, our center is large enough to feel lively and full of friends, yet small enough that every child is known by name.

What Makes Evanston West Special

The heart of our center is our outdoor nature classroom. Children here don't just learn about the natural world from books. They step outside to see it, touch it, and wonder about it. A fallen leaf becomes a science lesson. A puddle becomes an experiment. A quiet moment watching clouds becomes a conversation about weather and seasons. Time outdoors helps children build curiosity, coordination, and a real sense of care for the world around them.

Because we focus on children ages 2 to 5 years, our whole center is designed around the needs of toddlers and preschoolers. Please note that we do not offer infant care at Evanston West. That focus lets our teachers put their full attention on the years when children are learning to share, solve problems, use words to express big feelings, and get ready for kindergarten.

A Day That Works for Families

We know mornings and evenings can be a juggle. Our hours, Mon-Fri 7:30 AM - 6:00 PM, give families flexibility for work schedules and commutes. Free parking makes drop-off and pick-up simple, so you can spend those few minutes connecting with your child's teacher instead of circling the block.

Our Community

Under the leadership of Director Kevin Osei, Evanston West has grown into a place where families feel welcome and children feel at home.
