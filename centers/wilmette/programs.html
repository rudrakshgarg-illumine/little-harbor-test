Programs at Little Harbor Wilmette

At our Green Bay Road center, we offer three programs for children from 15 months to 5 years: Toddler, Twos and Preschool. Every classroom follows a Montessori approach. Spaces are prepared with care, materials sit on low shelves within reach, and children are encouraged to choose, explore and do for themselves. All programs run within our center hours of Mon-Fri 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM and are offered in English.

Toddler

Our youngest learners join us starting at 15 months, a time of enormous discovery. In the Toddler classroom, the day is gentle and predictable, with plenty of room to move, climb and explore. Teachers support early independence in small, meaningful ways: helping to put on shoes, carrying a cup to the table, returning a toy to its shelf. Simple Montessori materials invite toddlers to grasp, stack, open and close, building coordination and focus. Just as important, toddlers learn that school is a safe, warm place where grown-ups respond to their needs and celebrate their efforts.

Twos

Two-year-olds are bursting with language, opinions and energy, and our Twos classroom is designed to welcome all of it. Children practice practical life skills like pouring, wiping spills and washing hands, which build confidence along with fine motor control. Songs, stories and conversation help vocabulary grow by the day. Teachers guide children through their first experiences with sharing space, taking turns and naming feelings, always with patience. By the end of their time in Twos, many children are proudly managing routines that once seemed out of reach.

Preschool

In Preschool, children up to 5 years old dig deeper into the Montessori classroom. They work with materials that introduce early math concepts, letter sounds, sorting and sequencing, and the natural world, often choosing their own work and staying with it for long, focused stretches. Older children model for younger ones, and everyone learns to care for the classroom community they share. Preschoolers leave us with a love of learning, a strong sense of independence, and the social skills that help them feel ready for the next step.

Half-Day Preschool Option

For families who want a shorter day, we offer a half-day preschool option from 8:30 AM - 12:00 PM. Half-day preschoolers enjoy the same Montessori classroom and the core morning learning time, then head home for the afternoon. It's a lovely choice for children easing into school or families who want to keep afternoons open.
