Frequently Asked Questions – Wilmette

What ages do you serve?

Our Wilmette center cares for children from 15 months to 5 years, across our Toddler, Twos and Preschool programs.

What are your hours?

We are open Mon-Fri 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM.

Do you offer a half-day option?

Yes. We offer a half-day preschool option from 8:30 AM - 12:00 PM.

What is a Montessori classroom like?

Our Montessori classrooms are carefully prepared spaces where materials sit within children's reach and children are encouraged to choose their work, move freely and build independence. Teachers guide, observe and support rather than direct every moment.

Where are you located?

We're at 1115 Green Bay Road, Wilmette, IL 60091. Many families know us simply as "Green Bay Road."

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is available at our Wilmette center.

What language are your programs taught in?

Our programs at the Wilmette center are offered in English.

What is your sick policy?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center.

How can I schedule a tour?

Tours are offered on Thursdays at 1:00 PM and last 30 minutes. Call us at (847) 555-0124 to plan your visit.

Who is the director?

Hannah Brooks is the director of our Wilmette center. You can reach her and our team at (847) 555-0124.
