Little Harbor Early Learning – Wilmette

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning in Wilmette, known to many of our families simply as Green Bay Road. Since opening our doors in 2015, we have been a home base for curious toddlers and preschoolers on Chicago's North Shore. It's a place where small hands get busy with real work, where friendships form over shared snacks, and where every child is known by name.

A Montessori Approach, Close to Home

What makes our Wilmette center special is our Montessori classrooms. Each room is arranged so children can choose their work, move freely, and build independence one small success at a time. You'll see toddlers carefully carrying trays, twos pouring water at child-sized stations, and preschoolers deep in concentration over materials designed to help them explore, sort, count and create. We believe children learn best when they are trusted to try, and our classrooms are built around that belief.

Not every family needs a full day, so we also offer a half-day preschool option from 8:30 AM - 12:00 PM. It's a wonderful fit for children who are ready for a classroom morning and families who want afternoons at home.

Who We Serve

We care for children from 15 months to 5 years across three programs: Toddler, Twos and Preschool. With a capacity of 70 children, our center is big enough to offer a lively community and small enough that our director and teachers know every family. Our programs are offered in English.
