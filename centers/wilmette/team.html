Our Team at Green Bay Road

A school is only as good as the people in it. At Little Harbor Wilmette, our team shares one simple commitment: every child who walks through our doors at 1115 Green Bay Road should feel known, safe and capable.

Meet Our Director, Hannah Brooks

Hannah Brooks leads our Wilmette center. Hannah is the person you'll see greeting families, checking in on classrooms and answering questions about everything from first-day jitters to moving up to the next program. She works closely with our teachers to keep our Montessori classrooms thoughtful, calm and engaging, and she keeps the lines of communication open with parents. If you have a question or idea, Hannah's door is open, and you can always reach the center at (847) 555-0124.

Our Teachers

Our teachers are the heart of Green Bay Road. They are the kind of people who get down on the floor to see the room from a child's height, who notice when a toddler is about to master a new skill, and who know that patience is part of the curriculum.

In our Montessori classrooms, teachers act as guides rather than lecturers. They prepare the environment with care, show children how to use materials, and then step back to let children explore and solve problems on their own. They observe closely, offer encouragement at just the right moment, and help children build the independence that makes Montessori learning so meaningful.

Our teachers also care deeply about relationships, with children and with families. They share the small moments of the day, celebrate milestones with you, and partner with you when something feels hard. With a center capacity of 70 children, our team gets to know each family well, and many children grow up with familiar faces from their first Toddler days to their final year of Preschool.
