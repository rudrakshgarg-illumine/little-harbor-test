Little Harbor Early Learning

Tour Our Wilmette Center

Choosing an early learning center is a big decision, and nothing helps more than seeing a classroom in action. We warmly invite you to visit Little Harbor Wilmette at 1115 Green Bay Road.

Tour Times

Thursdays at 1:00 PM

Each tour lasts 30 minutes. To plan your visit, call us at (847) 555-0124.

What to Expect

  • A look inside our Montessori classrooms. You'll see how our Toddler, Twos and Preschool rooms are arranged to help children choose their work and build independence.
  • Time to ask questions. Bring your list, whether it's about daily routines, our hours of Mon-Fri 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM, or our half-day preschool option from 8:30 AM - 12:00 PM.
  • A chance to meet our team. Our director, Hannah Brooks, and our teachers are glad to share what a day at Green Bay Road looks like.
  • A feel for our community. With a capacity of 70 children, our center has a close-knit, welcoming atmosphere you can sense the moment you walk in.

Getting Here

Free parking is available at our center, so arriving for your tour is easy. You're welcome to bring your child along. Many little ones love peeking into the classrooms.

We look forward to meeting your family on a Thursday afternoon soon.
