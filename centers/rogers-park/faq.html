Frequently Asked Questions – Rogers Park

Where is the Rogers Park center located?

We are at 6620 N Sheridan Road, Chicago, IL 60626. Some families know us as our Sheridan Road center.

What are your hours?

We are open Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

What ages do you serve?

We care for children from 6 weeks to 5 years.

Which programs do you offer?

We offer four programs: Infant, Toddler, Twos, and Preschool.

Do you accept child care assistance?

Yes. We accept Illinois CCAP child care assistance. Please call us at (773) 555-0125 and we will be happy to talk through it with you.

What languages are spoken at the center?

Languages spoken at our Rogers Park center include English, Spanish, and Amharic.

Is there parking?

Yes. Our Rogers Park center offers free parking.

When can my child return after being sick?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center.

Who is the director?

Samuel Adeyemi is the director of our Rogers Park center.

How do I schedule a tour?

Tours are offered on Tuesdays at 3:00 PM and last 30 minutes. Call (773) 555-0125 to plan your visit.
