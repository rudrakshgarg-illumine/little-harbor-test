Meet Our Rogers Park Team

A great early learning center is built on people. At Little Harbor Rogers Park, our team is the reason families feel confident walking through our doors on Sheridan Road each morning and the reason children run to greet familiar faces.

Our Director: Samuel Adeyemi

Samuel Adeyemi leads our Rogers Park center. As director, Samuel is the person families can turn to with questions big and small, from first enrollment conversations to everyday check-ins. He works alongside our teachers to keep the center running smoothly, support each classroom, and make sure every child and family feels welcomed and known. Whether you are touring for the first time or have been with us for years, Samuel's door is open.

Our Teachers

Our teachers care for children from 6 weeks to 5 years across our Infant, Toddler, Twos, and Preschool programs. While each classroom looks different, the teachers who lead them share a common heart for young children.

  • They are warm and patient. Whether soothing a baby, guiding a toddler through a tough moment, or answering a preschooler's hundredth "why," our teachers meet children with kindness.
  • They know each child. With room for up to 90 children, we are a center where teachers learn every child's personality, preferences, and growing skills.
  • They partner with families. Teachers share updates, listen to family insights, and work together with parents and caregivers to support each child.
  • They reflect our community. Languages spoken at our center include English, Spanish, and Amharic, helping many families communicate comfortably and children feel at home.

Many of our families come from right here in Rogers Park, and our team takes pride in being part of this neighborhood's daily life. We have been caring for local children since 2017, and we are grateful for every family who trusts us.
