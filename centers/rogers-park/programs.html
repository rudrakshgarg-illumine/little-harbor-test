Programs at Little Harbor Rogers Park

At our Rogers Park center on Sheridan Road, we care for children from 6 weeks to 5 years through four programs: Infant, Toddler, Twos, and Preschool. Each program is shaped around what children need at that stage of life, and together they form a steady path from a family's first days of care all the way to kindergarten readiness. All programs run during our regular hours, Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

Infant

Our Infant program welcomes babies as young as 6 weeks. In these earliest months, everything begins with trust. Teachers get to know each baby's rhythms for feeding, napping, and comfort, and they work closely with families so that care at the center feels connected to care at home. Days are filled with cuddles, conversation, songs, tummy time, and gentle exploration of new sights, sounds, and textures. For many families, this is the first time leaving their baby in someone else's arms, and our team treats that responsibility with great tenderness.

Toddler

Once children are on the move, our Toddler program gives them room to explore safely. Toddlers are curious about everything, and teachers channel that energy into climbing, stacking, pouring, reading together, and simple group moments like songs and snack time. This is a season of big feelings and fast-growing language, so teachers help children name what they feel, practice taking turns, and discover the joy of doing things on their own.

Twos

Our Twos program is built for children who are bursting with words, ideas, and independence. Teachers support early friendships, simple routines, and lots of hands-on play with art, blocks, sensory materials, and pretend play. Children begin practicing self-help skills, following short sequences, and expressing themselves with more confidence. It is a joyful, busy year, and our teachers meet it with patience and encouragement.

Preschool

In Preschool, children up to 5 years old build the skills and confidence they will carry into kindergarten. Through play-based learning, they explore early literacy, numbers, science, art, music, and movement. Just as important, they practice listening, cooperating, solving problems, and caring for one another. Teachers notice each child's interests and use them to spark deeper learning and conversation.

Learning in Many Languages

Across every program, languages spoken at our center include English, Spanish, and Amharic, reflecting the vibrant Rogers Park community.
