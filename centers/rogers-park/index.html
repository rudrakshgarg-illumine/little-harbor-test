Little Harbor Early Learning – Rogers Park

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning in Rogers Park, known to many of our families simply as our Sheridan Road center. Since opening our doors in 2017, we have been a warm, steady place where babies, toddlers, and preschoolers can grow, explore, and feel at home every day of the week.

Our center serves up to 90 children, which means we are large enough to offer a full range of programs and small enough that every child is known by name. Families walk in each morning and are greeted by teachers who know their child's routines, their favorite songs, and the little things that make each day go smoothly.

What Makes Rogers Park Special

Rogers Park is one of Chicago's most diverse neighborhoods, and our center reflects that. Languages spoken here include English, Spanish, and Amharic, so many families can share questions, updates, and everyday conversations in the language that feels most like home. For young children, hearing familiar words and welcoming voices can make the transition into care feel a little gentler.

We are also proud to accept Illinois CCAP child care assistance. We believe high-quality early learning should be within reach for neighborhood families, and we are glad to talk through how CCAP can work for your family when you contact us.

With programs spanning from 6 weeks to 5 years, children can stay with us from their very first months through the year before kindergarten. That continuity matters: children build lasting friendships, families build relationships with our team, and each new classroom feels like a familiar next step rather than a brand-new beginning.
