Tour Little Harbor Rogers Park

Choosing care for your child is a big decision, and nothing helps more than seeing a center for yourself. We warmly invite you to visit our Rogers Park center at 6620 N Sheridan Road, Chicago, IL 60626.

Tour Times

  • When: Tue 3:00 PM
  • Length: 30 minutes
  • Parking: Free parking
  • To plan your visit: Call (773) 555-0125

What to Expect

During your 30-minute tour, you will walk through our center and see where children from 6 weeks to 5 years spend their days in our Infant, Toddler, Twos, and Preschool programs. You will get a real sense of daily life here and meet members of our team, possibly including our director, Samuel Adeyemi.

Bring your questions. Families often ask about our programs, how we support children through transitions, our sick policy, and how Illinois CCAP child care assistance works with enrollment. We are happy to talk through anything on your mind. Languages spoken at our center include English, Spanish, and Amharic, so please let us know if you would like to have your conversation in one of these languages.

You are welcome to bring your child along. Watching how your little one responds to a new space can be one of the most helpful parts of a visit.

We Look Forward to Meeting You

Our Rogers Park center has been part of this neighborhood since 2017, and welcoming new families is one of our favorite parts of the week. Call (773) 555-0125 to join us on a Tuesday at 3:00 PM.
