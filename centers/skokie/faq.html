Frequently Asked Questions – Skokie

What ages do you serve?

Our Skokie center serves children from 6 weeks to 6 years.

What are your hours?

We are open Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:30 PM.

What programs do you offer?

We offer Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, Pre-K, and Kindergarten programs. Skokie is the only Little Harbor center with a kindergarten.

Where are you located?

We are at 4910 Oakton Street, Skokie, IL 60077.

Is parking available?

Yes. Parking is free at our Skokie center.

What languages do your teachers speak?

Our team speaks English, Hindi, and Korean.

How long has the center been open?

Our Skokie center opened in 2012.

How many children attend the center?

The center has a capacity of 150 children across all six programs.

What is your sick policy?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center.

How can I schedule a tour?

Tours are offered Mon and Fri 11:00 AM and last 30 minutes. Call us at (847) 555-0123 to schedule your visit. Our director, Priya Raman, and her team look forward to meeting you.
