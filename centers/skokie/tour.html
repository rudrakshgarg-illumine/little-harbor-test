Tour Little Harbor Skokie

The best way to know whether a center feels right is to walk through the doors yourself. We would love to welcome you to Little Harbor Skokie and show you where children from 6 weeks to 6 years spend their days.

Tour Times

  • When: Mon and Fri 11:00 AM
  • Length: 30 minutes
  • Where: 4910 Oakton Street, Skokie, IL 60077
  • Parking: Free on site

To reserve your spot, call (847) 555-0123.

What to Expect

During your 30-minute visit, you will see our classrooms in action, from the calm, cozy Infant room to our Kindergarten. Skokie is the only Little Harbor center with a kindergarten. You will see how children can grow with us from their earliest months all the way to their first year of school.

You may have the chance to meet our director, Priya Raman, and some of the teachers who would care for your child. Our team speaks English, Hindi, and Korean, so feel free to ask questions in the language that feels most comfortable.

Bring your questions about daily routines, meals, naps, and how we partner with families. We will also walk you through practical details like our hours (Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:30 PM) and our sick policy, which requires children to be fever-free for 48 hours before returning.

Little ones are always welcome to come along. We can't wait to meet your family.
