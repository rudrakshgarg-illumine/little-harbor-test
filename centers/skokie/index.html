Little Harbor Early Learning – Skokie

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning in Skokie, where children have been learning, laughing, and growing together since 2012. Our center on Oakton Street serves families with children from 6 weeks to 6 years. We offer a warm, structured path from a baby's first months through the kindergarten year.

A Center Built for the Whole Early Childhood Journey

Skokie is the only Little Harbor center with a kindergarten. Families can start with us in our Infant room and stay through Kindergarten in the same familiar building, with the same caring community around them. For children, that continuity means fewer big transitions and more time with teachers and friends they already know and trust. For parents, it means one drop-off, one community, and one team that knows your child's story from the very beginning.

With space for 150 children, our center is large enough to offer six distinct programs: Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, Pre-K, and Kindergarten. It is still small enough that teachers know every child by name, and many know the older and younger siblings too.

A Multilingual Community

Skokie is one of the most diverse communities in the Chicago area, and our center reflects that. Our team speaks English, Hindi, and Korean. For many families, hearing a familiar language at drop-off makes those first weeks feel a little easier. Children also love picking up greetings, songs, and words from the languages their friends speak at home.

What Makes Skokie Special

Parents tell us what they love most is the sense that their child belongs here. Our center is led by Director Priya Raman. Her team keeps the days full of play, discovery, and gentle routine, and they share the details of each child's day with families. A baby may be taking first steps in our Infant room while down the hall a kindergartner is sounding out a first chapter book. Our Skokie center is a place where both milestones are celebrated.
