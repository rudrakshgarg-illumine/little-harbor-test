Little Harbor Early Learning

Meet the Skokie Team

A great early learning center is really about its people. At Little Harbor Skokie, our team welcomes children from 6 weeks to 6 years every day with patience, warmth, and a genuine delight in watching them grow.

Our Director: Priya Raman

Priya Raman leads our Skokie center. As director, she oversees all six programs, from Infant through Kindergarten. Her focus is making sure every family feels known and every child feels safe. Parents often find Priya at the front door at drop-off. She greets children by name, checks in with families, and keeps an eye on the small details that make a big difference in a child's day.

Priya believes the best learning happens when children feel secure and curious. She works closely with teachers to keep classrooms joyful and well organized. Her door is always open to parents who have a question, a concern, or a story to share about their child's latest accomplishment.

Our Teachers

Our Skokie teachers are the heart of the center. They are the people who rock babies to sleep, cheer on wobbly first steps, and kneel down to really listen when a preschooler has an idea. They also guide kindergartners through their first chapter books. Each teacher is matched to an age group, so their routines and activities fit the children in their care.

Our team speaks English, Hindi, and Korean. That reflects the families we serve and helps children and parents feel at home. A familiar language can make a hard goodbye a little easier, and it helps families and teachers understand each other clearly.

Because Skokie is the only Little Harbor center with a kindergarten, many of our teachers get to watch children grow over several years. They share notes as children move from room to room. That way, each new teacher already knows what comforts your child, what excites them, and what they are working on.
