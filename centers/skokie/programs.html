Little Harbor Early Learning

Programs at Little Harbor Skokie

Our Skokie center serves children from 6 weeks to 6 years across six programs. Each classroom is designed for its age group, with routines, materials, and expectations that grow along with your child. Because Skokie is the only Little Harbor center with a kindergarten, children can move through every stage of early learning here, from their first bottle to their first full year of school.

Infant

Our Infant program welcomes babies as young as 6 weeks. Teachers follow each baby's own rhythm for feeding, napping, and play, and they keep families updated throughout the day. The room is calm and cozy, with plenty of floor time, soft books, singing, and close one-on-one attention. This is where trust begins, for babies and for parents.

Toddler

Toddlers are on the move, and our Toddler classroom is built for exploring. Children climb, stack, scribble, splash, and discover what their bodies and voices can do. Teachers support early language with songs, simple stories, and lots of conversation. They also help toddlers take their first steps toward sharing, taking turns, and managing big feelings.

Twos

Two-year-olds are bursting with independence, and our Twos program gives them room to practice. Children help with simple classroom jobs, try out new words every day, and dive into sensory play, art, and pretend play. Teachers also support toilet learning in close partnership with families. Predictable routines help twos feel secure while they test their growing skills.

Preschool

In Preschool, curiosity takes center stage. Children explore early math through counting games and building, listen to and retell stories, and make big discoveries through hands-on projects. Group time, dramatic play, and outdoor play help preschoolers build friendships and learn to work together.

Pre-K

Pre-K gets children ready for the step into kindergarten. Children practice early literacy skills like letter recognition and sounds, work with numbers and patterns, and build the focus and independence they will need in a classroom. Just as important, they learn to listen to others, solve problems, and express themselves with confidence.

Kindergarten

Our Kindergarten is unique among Little Harbor centers. Children build on everything they have learned so far with reading, writing, math, and inquiry-based projects, in small, nurturing classes. Many of our kindergartners have been with us since they were babies. They begin their school years in a familiar place, surrounded by teachers who know them well.

A Multilingual Setting

Across all six programs, children benefit from a team that speaks English, Hindi, and Korean. Families who speak these languages at home often find that a familiar word or song helps their child settle in.
