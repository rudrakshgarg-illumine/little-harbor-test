Tour Little Harbor Naperville

Choosing care for your child is a big decision, and nothing helps quite like seeing a center in person. We invite you to visit our Route 59 center, meet our team, and picture your child's day with us.

Tour Times

Tue and Thu 2:00 PM

Each tour lasts 30 minutes, so it fits easily into a lunch break or a busy afternoon. To plan your visit, call us at (630) 555-0129.

What to Expect

  • A warm welcome. You'll be greeted at the front door and introduced to our center, which has been part of the Naperville community since 2018.
  • A look at our classrooms. See our Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, Pre-K, and Before and after school spaces and get a feel for daily routines.
  • Time for your questions. Ask about hours, programs, the bus to Fry and Peterson elementary, or anything else on your mind. If our director, Laura Novak, is available, she'll be glad to say hello.
  • Helpful details. We'll share information about our hours, Mon-Fri 6:45 AM - 6:15 PM, and our sick policy, which requires children to be fever-free for 48 hours before returning.

Getting Here

Find us at 2860 Route 59, Naperville, IL 60564. Free parking is available at the center, so arriving for your tour is simple.

We look forward to showing you around and getting to know your family.
