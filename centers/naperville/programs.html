Programs at Little Harbor Naperville

At our Route 59 center, we care for children from 6 weeks to 6 years, and we offer before and after school care with bus service to Fry and Peterson elementary. Each program is built around the needs of children at that stage, with warm teachers, predictable routines, and plenty of room to play, explore, and learn. All programs run during our center hours, Mon-Fri 6:45 AM - 6:15 PM.

Infant

Our Infant classroom welcomes babies as young as 6 weeks. Here, care follows each baby's own rhythm for feeding, napping, and play. Teachers spend the day talking, singing, and reading with infants, building the trust and early language that matter so much in the first year. Families receive regular updates so you always feel connected to your baby's day.

Toddler

Toddlers are on the move, and our Toddler classroom is ready for them. Teachers encourage walking, climbing, stacking, and exploring in a safe, cheerful space. Simple routines help toddlers feel secure, while songs, stories, and sensory play support new words and growing independence.

Twos

Our Twos classroom celebrates this big, busy year. Children practice taking turns, using words to share feelings, and helping with everyday tasks like cleaning up and getting ready for outdoor play. Hands-on activities with art, music, and building invite twos to try new things and take pride in doing it themselves.

Preschool

In Preschool, curiosity leads the way. Children explore early math, letters, and science through play, projects, and conversation. Teachers support friendships, problem-solving, and self-help skills, giving preschoolers a strong foundation for the year ahead. With English, Mandarin, and Spanish spoken at our center, children also hear and appreciate the languages of their classmates and teachers.

Pre-K

Our Pre-K program helps children get ready for kindergarten with confidence. Activities build early reading and writing, number sense, focus, and the social skills children need in a classroom. Just as importantly, Pre-K children learn to listen, ask questions, and believe in their own ability to learn.

Before and After School

Our Before and after school program gives school-age children a friendly, familiar place to start and end their school day. We provide a bus to Fry and Peterson elementary, making mornings and afternoons simpler for families. Before school, children can ease into the day; after school, they can unwind, play, and connect with friends until pick-up.
