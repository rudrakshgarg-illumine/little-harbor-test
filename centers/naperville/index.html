Little Harbor Early Learning – Naperville

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning in Naperville, known to many local families simply as our Route 59 center. Since opening our doors in 2018, we have been a place where babies take their first wobbly steps, toddlers discover new words, and preschoolers grow into confident, curious kids ready for kindergarten and beyond.

Our center is licensed for 140 children. That size allows us to offer a full range of classrooms, from infants through before and after school care, while every family still gets to know the people who care for their child each day.

What Makes Our Naperville Center Special

  • Bus service to Fry and Peterson elementary. Our before and after school program includes a bus to Fry and Peterson elementary, so families with school-age siblings can keep drop-off and pick-up in one familiar place.
  • A multilingual community. English, Mandarin, and Spanish are all spoken at our center, helping many children and families feel at home from the first day.
  • A full path of programs. Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, Pre-K, and Before and after school classrooms mean your child can grow with us year after year.
  • Experienced local leadership. Our center director, Laura Novak, leads a team that knows Naperville families and cares deeply about every child in the building.
