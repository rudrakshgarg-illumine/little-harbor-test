Meet Our Naperville Team

A great early learning center comes down to people. At Little Harbor Naperville, our team shows up every morning ready to greet children by name, comfort a teary goodbye, and celebrate every small milestone along the way.

Laura Novak, Center Director

Laura Novak leads our Route 59 center with warmth, steady organization, and a genuine love for young children. As director, Laura is the person families can turn to with questions about enrollment, classrooms, daily routines, or anything on their minds. She works closely with our teachers to keep every classroom, from Infant to Before and after school, running smoothly and feeling like home.

Laura believes strong partnerships with families make all the difference. Whether you stop by at drop-off or call the center at (630) 555-0129, she is happy to talk and to help you feel confident about your child's day.

Our Teachers

Our teachers are the heart of Little Harbor Naperville. They are patient, attentive, and joyful, and they understand that children learn best when they feel safe and loved. In our infant rooms, that means gentle, responsive care. In our toddler and twos rooms, it means lots of encouragement as children try new things. In Preschool and Pre-K, it means guiding curious minds toward kindergarten readiness. And in our Before and after school program, it means being a reliable, friendly presence at the start and end of the school day.

Our team reflects the community we serve. English, Mandarin, and Spanish are spoken at our center, which helps many children and families communicate comfortably and feel truly welcomed.

A Team Families Can Count On

Since opening in 2018, our Naperville team has built lasting relationships with local families, many of whom stay with us as children move from one classroom to the next. We would love for you to meet our team in person. Tours are offered Tue and Thu 2:00 PM and last 30 minutes.
