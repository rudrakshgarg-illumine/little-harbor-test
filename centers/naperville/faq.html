Frequently Asked Questions – Naperville

What are your hours?

Our Naperville center is open Mon-Fri 6:45 AM - 6:15 PM.

What ages do you serve?

We care for children from 6 weeks to 6 years, and we also offer a Before and after school program.

What programs do you offer?

We offer Infant, Toddler, Twos, Preschool, Pre-K, and Before and after school programs.

Where is the center located?

We are at 2860 Route 59, Naperville, IL 60564. Many families call us the Route 59 center.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available at our Naperville center.

Do you provide transportation to elementary schools?

Yes. Our Before and after school program includes a bus to Fry and Peterson elementary.

What languages are spoken at the center?

English, Mandarin, and Spanish are spoken at our center.

Who is the center director?

Laura Novak is the director of our Naperville center.

What is your sick policy?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center. You can find the full sick policy on our website.

How can I schedule a tour?

Tours are offered Tue and Thu 2:00 PM and last 30 minutes. Call us at (630) 555-0129 to plan your visit.
