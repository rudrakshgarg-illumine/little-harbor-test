Little Harbor Early Learning

Tour Little Harbor Logan Square

Choosing an early learning center is a big decision, and nothing compares to seeing a place for yourself. We warmly invite you to visit our Logan Square center at 2550 N Milwaukee Avenue and get a feel for daily life here.

Tour Times

  • Saturday: 10:00 AM
  • Tuesday: 4:00 PM

Each tour lasts 30 minutes, so it fits easily into a busy family schedule.

What to Expect

During your visit, you'll walk through our classrooms and see where children play, create, eat, and rest. You'll learn about our Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Saturday drop-in programs and how each supports children from 18 months to 5 years.

A Saturday tour is a great chance to see our drop-in program in action, since we're the only Little Harbor center open on Saturdays. A Tuesday afternoon tour offers a glimpse of the end of a busy weekday.

Bring your questions! We're happy to talk about daily routines, how we communicate with families, our sick policy, and the English, Spanish, and Polish voices that make our community special. You may also have the chance to meet our director, Diego Ramirez.

Getting Here

Free parking is available, so arriving is easy. Children are welcome to join you.

Questions before your visit? Call us at (773) 555-0127. We can't wait to meet your family.
