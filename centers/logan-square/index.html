Little Harbor Early Learning – Logan Square

Welcome to Little Harbor Logan Square, the center many of our families simply call "Milwaukee Avenue." Since opening our doors in 2019, we've been a warm, busy, joyful place where young children learn through play, friendship, and plenty of curiosity. With room for 80 children, we're big enough to offer a full range of programs and small enough that every child is known by name.

Who We Serve

We care for children from 18 months to 5 years. Families can grow with us from the toddler years through preschool, so children build lasting relationships with teachers and classmates along the way.

Hours

  • Monday – Friday: 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM

What Makes Logan Square Special

Logan Square is the only Little Harbor center open on Saturdays. Our Saturday drop-in program runs from 9 AM - 12 PM, giving families a little breathing room for errands, appointments, work shifts, or a quiet cup of coffee.

Our community also reflects the neighborhood around us. Here you'll hear English, Spanish, and Polish, and we love that children grow up surrounded by different languages, songs, and stories. Families often tell us that hearing a familiar language at drop-off makes the start of the day feel like home.

Every day brings a balance of active play, hands-on exploration, stories, music, and the small routines that help young children feel safe and confident: greeting friends, washing hands, helping tidy up, and sharing what they discovered.
