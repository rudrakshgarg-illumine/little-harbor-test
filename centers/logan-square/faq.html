Frequently Asked Questions – Logan Square

Where is the Logan Square center located?

We're at 2550 N Milwaukee Avenue, Chicago, IL 60647. Many families know us as the "Milwaukee Avenue" center.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM.

What ages do you serve?

We care for children from 18 months to 5 years.

What programs do you offer?

We offer Toddler, Twos, Preschool, and Saturday drop-in programs.

Are you really open on Saturdays?

Yes! Logan Square is the only Little Harbor center open on Saturdays. Our Saturday drop-in program runs from 9 AM - 12 PM.

What languages are spoken at the center?

English, Spanish, and Polish are all part of our community at Logan Square.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available at our Logan Square center.

What is your sick policy?

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours before returning to the center. This helps keep all of our children, families, and teachers healthy.

How big is the center?

Our Logan Square center has a capacity of 80 children. We've been part of the neighborhood since opening in 2019.

How can I schedule a tour?

Tours are offered Saturday at 10:00 AM and Tuesday at 4:00 PM, and each lasts 30 minutes. Call us at (773) 555-0127 to learn more.
