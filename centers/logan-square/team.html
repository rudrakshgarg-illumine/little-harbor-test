Our Team at Logan Square

The heart of any early learning center is its people. At Little Harbor Logan Square, our team is made up of caring, attentive educators who show up every day ready to listen, laugh, and help children grow.

Meet Our Director: Diego Ramirez

Diego Ramirez leads our Logan Square center. Diego sets the tone for a place where children feel safe and families feel welcome. You'll often find him greeting families at the door, checking in with teachers, and stopping to admire a block tower or a fresh painting. He believes that strong partnerships between families and teachers make all the difference, and he's always glad to talk through questions, celebrate milestones, or help with a tricky transition.

If you'd like to speak with Diego, call the center at (773) 555-0127 or say hello during one of our tours.

Our Teachers

Our teachers are the kind of people children run to at drop-off. They are patient, playful, and deeply interested in each child's ideas. They know that a toddler learning to pour water and a preschooler sounding out a word are both doing important work, and they give every moment the attention it deserves.

Our classrooms reflect the community around us. English, Spanish, and Polish are all part of daily life at our center, and our team embraces the languages and traditions families bring with them. Children see their home cultures honored and get to learn about their friends' cultures too.

Our teachers also value communication with families. You can expect to hear about your child's day: what made them laugh, what they tried for the first time, and what they're curious about next. When challenges come up, our teachers approach them with warmth and work with you as a team.

A Team That Feels Like Family

Since 2019, our Logan Square team has built a community where children, families, and teachers know and care about one another. We'd love for you to come meet us. Tours are offered Saturday at 10:00 AM and Tuesday at 4:00 PM.
