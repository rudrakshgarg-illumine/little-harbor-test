Programs at Little Harbor Logan Square

At Logan Square, we offer four programs for children from 18 months to 5 years. Each one is designed around how young children actually learn: by moving, touching, asking questions, and trying again. Here's a closer look at each.

Toddler

Our Toddler program welcomes our youngest learners, starting at 18 months. Toddlers are discovering their independence at lightning speed, and our classrooms are set up to support that safely. Children explore sensory materials, stack and build, listen to short stories, and sing songs together. Teachers focus on gentle routines that help toddlers feel secure, like predictable mealtimes, rest, and lots of outdoor and movement time. Along the way, children practice early words, simple choices, and the beginnings of playing alongside friends.

Twos

Two-year-olds are full of big feelings and big ideas, and our Twos program gives them room for both. Children build language through conversation, picture books, and songs. They begin practicing self-help skills like putting on a jacket or serving a snack. Art, pretend play, and building projects invite creativity, while teachers guide children in naming emotions, taking turns, and solving small problems with friends. It's a year of tremendous growth, and we celebrate every step.

Preschool

Our Preschool program prepares children for the next big adventure while protecting the joy of being little. Preschoolers dive into longer projects, early literacy and number play, science experiments, and dramatic play that can run for days. They practice listening in a group, sharing ideas, and working together. Because our community includes English, Spanish, and Polish speakers, children often encounter new words and songs from classmates, which sparks wonderful conversations about family, culture, and language. By the time they're ready to move on at 5, children leave with confidence, curiosity, and strong friendships.

Saturday Drop-In

Logan Square is the only Little Harbor center open on Saturdays. Our Saturday drop-in program runs from 9 AM - 12 PM and gives families flexible care when they need it most. Whether you have a work shift, an appointment, or a few errands to run, your child can spend the morning playing, creating, and making friends in a welcoming classroom. Saturday mornings have a relaxed, cheerful feel, with art, stories, and plenty of play.
