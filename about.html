Little Harbor Early Learning

About Little Harbor Early Learning

Little Harbor Early Learning began in 2009 with a simple belief: young children deserve a place that feels as safe and as loving as home, and families deserve partners they can trust completely. That belief has guided every decision we have made since, from the first classroom we set up to the tenth center we opened.

Where We Started

In 2009, Little Harbor opened its doors with a small group of families and a team of teachers who cared deeply about early childhood. Those first years taught us what matters most. Children thrive when adults slow down enough to really see them. Parents feel at ease when they know what their child's day looked like. And teachers do their best work when they are supported, respected and given room to know each child well.

Word spread the way it often does among families: at the playground, at the pediatrician's office, over coffee. Parents told other parents. As more families asked for a Little Harbor in their own neighborhoods, we grew carefully, one center at a time.

Growing Without Losing Our Heart

Today, Little Harbor is a group of 10 early learning centers near Chicago: Evanston, Evanston West, Skokie, Wilmette, Rogers Park, Lincoln Park, Logan Square, Oak Park, Naperville and Aurora. Each one belongs to its community. A morning in Logan Square sounds different from a morning in Naperville, and we like it that way. Our teachers bring the character of their neighborhoods into their classrooms, and families help shape each center's traditions.

Growth has never changed the fundamentals. We still keep our ratios small: Infants 1:4, toddlers 1:5, twos 1:6, preschool 1:10. We still believe a shared meal is part of learning, which is why breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack are included; menus are nut-free. And we still believe parents should never have to wonder how the day is going, so parents get daily photos and reports in the Little Harbor app.

What We Believe

  • Play is serious work. Children learn language, problem-solving and kindness through building, pretending, moving and making.
  • Relationships come first. A child who feels secure with their teacher is a child who is ready to explore.
  • Health and safety are non-negotiable. Our clear policies, including that children return fever-free for 48 hours, protect every child and every teacher.
  • Families are partners. You know your child best. We listen, share and work alongside you.
  • Teachers matter. The heart of every center is the people in it, and we invest in them.

Looking Ahead

More than a decade after we began, the work feels just as meaningful as it did in 2009. Every year brings new infants settling into their first classroom, toddlers finding their words, and preschoolers heading off to kindergarten with backpacks that look far too big for them. We are honored to be part of those years.
