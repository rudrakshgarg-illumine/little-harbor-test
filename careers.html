Careers at Little Harbor

Some people are simply good with young children. They notice the quiet toddler in the corner. They can turn a spilled cup of water into a science lesson. They know that a hard goodbye at the door is a big moment, not a small one. If that sounds like you, we would love to meet you.

Why Teach With Us

Since 2009, Little Harbor Early Learning has grown into a group of 10 early learning centers near Chicago: Evanston, Evanston West, Skokie, Wilmette, Rogers Park, Lincoln Park, Logan Square, Oak Park, Naperville and Aurora. Through all that growth, one thing has stayed true: our teachers are the heart of everything we do.

  • Small ratios that let you teach. Infants 1:4, toddlers 1:5, twos 1:6, preschool 1:10. You will have the time to truly know each child.
  • A real team. You will work alongside colleagues who share ideas, cover for each other and celebrate each other's wins.
  • Meaningful family partnerships. Parents get daily photos and reports in the Little Harbor app, and your observations help families feel connected to their child's day.
  • Room to grow. With 10 centers, there are many paths to build your career and take on new responsibilities over time.
  • Thoughtful time off together. Our centers close for New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the Friday after, December 24 and 25.

Who We Look For

We welcome people at many stages: experienced lead teachers, assistant teachers, and caring people who are ready to start a career in early childhood. Above all, we look for warmth, patience, reliability and genuine curiosity about how children learn. We value teammates who communicate kindly with families and who take health and safety seriously every single day.

A Day in the Classroom

Your day might include rocking an infant to sleep, guiding twos through their first art project, reading aloud to a circle of preschoolers, or sitting down for breakfast, lunch or an afternoon snack with the children you care for. No two days are the same, and every one of them matters.
