Little Harbor Early Learning

Welcome to Little Harbor Early Learning

Every morning, families across Chicago and its suburbs walk through our doors with coffee in one hand and a small, mittened hand in the other. Some children run straight to the block corner. Some need one more hug at the cubby. Our teachers know both kinds of mornings, and they meet every child exactly where they are. That is what Little Harbor has always been about: a safe, steady place where young children can explore, rest, play and grow.

Ten Centers, One Harbor

Little Harbor Early Learning is a group of 10 early learning centers, each rooted in its own neighborhood and each sharing the same commitment to warm, attentive care. Wherever your family lives or works, there is likely a Little Harbor nearby:

  • Evanston
  • Evanston West
  • Skokie
  • Wilmette
  • Rogers Park
  • Lincoln Park
  • Logan Square
  • Oak Park
  • Naperville
  • Aurora

Each center has its own personality, shaped by the families and teachers who fill it. What stays the same from Evanston to Aurora is the way we care for children: with patience, curiosity and a deep respect for how much learning happens in the early years.

Care You Can See

We care for infants, toddlers, twos and preschoolers in classrooms built for their stage of life. Our ratios are small on purpose: Infants 1:4, toddlers 1:5, twos 1:6, preschool 1:10. That means your child is known, not just counted.

Breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack are included; menus are nut-free. And because the hours apart can feel long, parents get daily photos and reports in the Little Harbor app, so you can see the painting, the nap, the new friend, as the day unfolds.

A Place to Grow Together

Children here learn through play: building, singing, splashing paint, reading the same favorite book for the fifth time. Our teachers notice the small milestones and celebrate them with you. We believe families are partners, and we work hard to make every conversation at pickup feel like it matters, because it does.

Come See for Yourself

The best way to understand Little Harbor is to visit. Call our main number at (847) 555-0100, or reach out to the center closest to you through our contact page. We would love to show you around, introduce you to our teachers, and answer every question you bring.

Welcome to the harbor. We are glad you are here.
