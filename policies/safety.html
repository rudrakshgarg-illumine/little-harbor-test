Safety and Pickup Policy

When you leave your child at Little Harbor Early Learning, you are trusting us with the most important person in your world. We take that trust seriously. Our pickup policy is one of the simplest and most important ways we keep children safe, and we follow it every day, for every child, without exception.

Who May Pick Up Your Child

Only adults listed on the pickup list may collect a child. When you enroll, you will tell us which adults are authorized to pick up your child. These might be parents, grandparents, other relatives, neighbors, or trusted friends. If a person is not on the list, our teachers will not release your child to them, even if they seem familiar or say they have your permission.

We know this can occasionally feel inconvenient, especially when plans change at the last minute. But this rule exists to protect your child, and we hold to it firmly. Please keep your list up to date so the right people can always pick up your child when you need them to.

Photo ID on the First Visit

Photo ID is checked the first time. The first time an authorized adult comes to pick up your child, our staff will ask to see a photo ID and confirm that the person matches someone on your pickup list. This helps our teachers get to know each of the trusted adults in your child's life.

Please let the adults on your list know to bring their photo ID the first time they pick up, so the process goes smoothly and your child can head home without delay.

Keeping Your List Current

  • Add any new adults to your pickup list before they come to collect your child.
  • Remove anyone who should no longer pick up your child.
  • Talk with your center director if you have questions or special circumstances to discuss.

A Shared Responsibility

Safety at Little Harbor goes beyond pickup. Our teachers supervise children closely throughout the day, in classrooms, during play, and through every transition. Still, pickup is a moment when clear communication between families and staff matters most.

When an adult arrives, our teachers make sure the hand-off is calm and deliberate. Children learn that pickup is a predictable routine, and families can feel confident that their child is going home only with someone they trust.

If you ever have a concern about your child's safety, or about who may be picking up your child, please speak with your center director right away. We are here to listen and to help.

Thank you for partnering with us to keep every Little Harbor child safe.
