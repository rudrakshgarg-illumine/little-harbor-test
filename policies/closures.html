Little Harbor Early Learning

Holidays and Closures

We know families plan carefully around their child care, and we want you to have the information you need well in advance. This page explains when Little Harbor Early Learning centers are closed, how we communicate weather closures, and the monthly training closure at our Oak Park center.

Holiday Closures

All Little Harbor centers are closed on the following days:

  • New Year's Day
  • Memorial Day
  • July 4
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving and the Friday after
  • December 24 and 25

These days give our teachers time to rest and celebrate with their own families, so they can return refreshed and ready to give your child their very best. We encourage families to mark these dates on the calendar early and make arrangements ahead of time.

Snow Closures

Chicago winters can be unpredictable. When snow or severe weather makes it unsafe for families and staff to travel, we may need to close. Snow closures are announced in the app by 6:00 AM.

On snowy mornings, please check the app before you head out the door. Making the announcement by 6:00 AM gives families time to adjust their plans before the workday begins. We never take the decision to close lightly, and we only close when we believe it is the safest choice for children, families, and teachers.

  • Make sure the app is installed and notifications are turned on.
  • Check the app on any morning with heavy snow or dangerous weather.
  • Have a backup care plan ready for winter, just in case.

Oak Park Staff Training Day

Oak Park closes the first Monday of each month for staff training. On these days, our Oak Park teachers come together to learn, plan, and grow in their work with young children. Ongoing training helps our team stay current in early childhood practices, strengthen classroom routines, and bring fresh ideas back to your child's day.

If your child attends Oak Park, please plan for care on the first Monday of every month. This closure applies to our Oak Park center only.

Questions About Closures

If you have questions about an upcoming closure, or need help thinking through your plans, please reach out to your center director. We appreciate your flexibility and understanding, and we are grateful for the families who help make these days work. Our goal is always to keep you informed so there are no surprises, and so your child can look forward to coming back to a classroom full of rested, prepared, and happy teachers.
