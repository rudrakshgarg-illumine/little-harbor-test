Tuition and Billing

Choosing child care is one of the biggest decisions a family makes, and understanding the cost should be clear and simple. At Little Harbor Early Learning, we aim to make billing easy and transparent so you can focus on what matters most: your child's growth and happiness.

Monthly Billing Through the App

Tuition is billed monthly through the app. Rather than juggling paper invoices or checks, families can view and manage their tuition right from the same app they use to stay connected with their child's center.

Billing through the app means you can:

  • See your monthly tuition statement in one convenient place.
  • Keep track of your billing history over time.
  • Stay organized alongside other center updates and announcements.

If you need help setting up the app or have questions about a statement, your center director is happy to walk you through it.

Sibling Discount

Little Harbor offers a 10% sibling discount. We love when brothers and sisters grow up in our centers together, and we know that care for more than one child is a significant commitment for a family. The 10% sibling discount is our way of supporting families who trust us with more than one child.

If you have more than one child enrolled, or are planning to enroll a sibling, please speak with your center director to learn how the discount applies to your family.

Current Rates

Families should call their center for current rates. Tuition can vary depending on your child's classroom and your center, so the best way to get accurate, up-to-date information is to contact the Little Harbor center you are interested in directly.

When you call, our team can answer your questions about tuition, explain how billing works, and help you understand what is included in your child's day. For example, breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack are included for every child.

What Your Tuition Supports

Your tuition supports caring, trained teachers who know your child by name, thoughtful classrooms filled with opportunities to explore, and the daily routines that help children feel safe and confident. It also supports ongoing teacher training, so our staff can keep learning and bringing their best to your child's classroom.

We Are Here to Help

We understand that every family's situation is different. If you have questions about tuition, billing, or the sibling discount, please do not hesitate to reach out to your center. Our directors are glad to talk with you, answer your questions honestly, and help you decide whether Little Harbor is the right fit for your family. We look forward to welcoming you.
