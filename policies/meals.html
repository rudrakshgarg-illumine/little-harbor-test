Little Harbor Early Learning

Meals and Nutrition

Good food fuels good days. At Little Harbor Early Learning, mealtimes are about much more than eating. They are a chance for children to try new tastes, practice independence, build table manners, and enjoy conversation with friends and teachers. We want every child to feel nourished, comfortable, and included at the table.

What Is Included

Breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack are included. Families do not need to pack these meals. Your child will be offered each of them as part of their regular day at Little Harbor, so mornings at home can be a little less rushed and you can trust that your child is eating well while in our care.

  • Breakfast helps children start the day with energy for learning and play.
  • Lunch is a time to gather together, sit down, and refuel at the midpoint of the day.
  • Afternoon snack gives children a boost to carry them through the rest of their time with us.

Our Nut-Free Commitment

Menus are nut-free. Nut allergies can be serious, and many children are affected. By keeping our menus nut-free, we create a safer environment where every child can eat alongside their classmates without worry.

To support this commitment, we ask that families help us keep nuts out of our centers. If you ever send in a treat for a special occasion, please check with your center first so we can make sure it fits our nut-free approach.

Children With Allergies

If your child has a food allergy, a signed action plan from your child's doctor is required before the first day, as described in our health policy. Please also talk with your center director about your child's needs so our teachers understand how to support your child at every meal and snack.

Mealtime as Learning Time

Our teachers sit with children during meals, modeling healthy habits and encouraging conversation. Children are gently invited to try new foods, but never pressured. We believe a relaxed, positive mealtime helps children develop a healthy relationship with food that lasts a lifetime.

Depending on their stage, children may practice using utensils, pouring, passing dishes, or cleaning up their own spaces. These small moments of independence build confidence and coordination.

Questions About Menus

If you have questions about what your child is eating, or if you would like to share your child's preferences or cultural or family food traditions, please speak with your center director. We are always glad to partner with families to make mealtimes a warm and welcoming part of the Little Harbor day.
