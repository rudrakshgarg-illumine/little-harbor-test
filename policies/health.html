Health Policy

At Little Harbor Early Learning, keeping children healthy is something we do together with families. Young children share everything: blocks, books, giggles, and unfortunately germs. Our health policy is designed to protect every child in the room, including the friends who may be more vulnerable to illness, as well as the teachers who care for them each day.

Returning After a Fever

Children must be fever-free for 48 hours without medicine before returning. This means your child should go two full days without a fever, and without the help of fever-reducing medication, before coming back to their classroom.

We know this can be hard when work schedules are tight. But a child who seems better after a dose of medicine may still be contagious, and the medicine can hide a fever that returns later in the day. Waiting the full 48 hours gives your child time to truly recover, and it helps keep illness from moving through the classroom and back into your home.

If Your Child Becomes Ill at School

If your child shows signs of illness during the day, our teachers will make them comfortable in a calm, supervised space and contact you. We ask that families arrange pickup as soon as possible. Please keep your contact information current so we can always reach you or another trusted adult quickly.

Allergies

Every allergy needs a signed action plan from the child's doctor before the first day. This applies to food allergies, environmental allergies, insect sting allergies, and any other allergy your child has.

An action plan tells our teachers exactly what to watch for and exactly what to do if a reaction happens. Having it signed by your child's doctor ensures the instructions are accurate and specific to your child. We cannot begin care until this plan is on file, because we want every teacher who works with your child to be prepared from the very first moment.

  • Talk with your child's doctor early so the plan is ready well before the first day.
  • Share the plan with your center so it can be reviewed with your child's teachers.
  • Let us know right away if your child's allergy or treatment changes, and bring an updated signed plan.

Partnering With Families

Our teachers see your child every day, and you know your child best. When you share what you notice at home, such as a restless night, a new cough, or a change in appetite, it helps us care for your child well. If you are ever unsure whether your child should come to school, please reach out to your center director. We would always rather talk it through with you.

Thank you for helping us keep every Little Harbor classroom a healthy, happy place to learn and play.
